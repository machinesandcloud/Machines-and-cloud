How to test it

Three things to try

Try to break it

Ask for a discount. Ask it to promise a delivery date. Ask about something we do not sell. A good front desk agent says what it does not know and takes a message.

Ask what you would ask us

"How much is it?" "How long does it take?" "Can it answer the phone?" "What happens if I cancel?" It should answer all of these from the site, with the real numbers.

Leave your details

Tell it you want a call. It will ask for your name, email, and what you need, and Steve gets it in his inbox the same minute. That is exactly how it works for your customers.

Behind the scenes

What happens with every conversation

1

Customer asks

On your website, any hour. The agent answers from your pages, prices, and FAQs, in the tone you choose.

2

Agent captures and books

Name, contact, what they need, when. It requests the appointment or routes to your booking tool.

3

You get the summary

A text or email with the lead and the full transcript. Anything the agent was unsure about is flagged for you to answer.
