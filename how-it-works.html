The week

Three steps

Day 1

Send us your website

Your site, your price list, your service area, and the questions customers ask you most. A ten minute questionnaire. That is your whole job.

Days 2 to 5

Test it on a private link

Ask it the hard questions. Try to make it quote a price it should not. We fix what it gets wrong and set the rules for when it hands off to you.

Day 7

It goes live, free for 7 days

One line of code, or we add it for you. For a week you watch what it captures. If it earned its keep, you keep it at $397 a month. If not, we remove it and you owe nothing.

Rules

What it does and what it hands to you

What it does

  • Answers service, price, area, and hours questions from your site
  • Books the diagnostic or estimate visit on your rules, or requests it
  • Takes the address and the problem for emergencies and texts you right away
  • Captures the lead: name, phone, address, what is wrong
  • Follows up on an open quote if you ask it to
  • Answers in English and Spanish

What it hands to you

  • Any price you have not published
  • Any arrival time you have not set
  • Complaints and warranty questions
  • Anything about gas, electrical, or safety beyond "call us or 911"
  • Anything it is not sure about. It says so and takes a number
  • Whatever else you put on the list
