1. About this agreement

You accept it when you create a workspace, check the agreement box before paying, or use the service. The person accepting confirms that they are at least 18 and are authorized to bind the business. Our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms explain how we handle information and text messages.

"Your customers" means the people who contact your business through the service. "Customer data" means the information about your customers and their conversations that the service handles for you.

2. What the service does

Machines & Cloud is a technology platform. You set up a workspace with your business details, hours, services, prices and rules, and you choose which channels to turn on: website chat, phone answering, text messaging, Google Calendar, and lead intake from Thumbtack or Angi.

Once you turn a channel on, the software acts automatically on your behalf, based on your saved settings:

  • An AI model writes and sends replies to your customers in website chat, by text and on phone calls, using your saved details and rules
  • It saves visit requests and leads, checks availability, and emails you notifications
  • For customers who agreed in writing to appointment texts, it sends a confirmation when a visit is saved or cancelled and reminders about 24 hours and 1 hour before the arrival window
  • It asks a customer for permission before ongoing appointment texts or follow-ups, using wording we provide
  • If connected, it creates, updates and deletes Google Calendar events for saved visits

Texts and calls go out in your business's name, from the dedicated number registered to your business. You decide whether each channel is on, what it says about your business, and when to pause it.

3. Availability and what is not live yet

Your workspace shows the status of each channel. A channel is available to your customers only when it shows as live. Phone answering and text messaging need a dedicated number, registration of your business with the carriers, and approval by our providers and the carriers. Thumbtack and Angi need access from those platforms. These third parties decide on their own timeline and can refuse or withdraw approval, so we cannot promise a date. We do not promise that the service will be uninterrupted or error free.

Daily AI usage limits apply and are shown in your workspace. When a limit is reached, the front desk stops answering on that channel until the limit resets at midnight Houston time. Setting up a workspace is free.

You are responsible for activity in your workspace and for keeping your details accurate. Tell us promptly at support@machinesandcloud.com if you suspect unauthorized access. Our staff can access your workspace to provide support, keep the service secure, and meet legal obligations.

Paid plans are Coverage at $99 per month and Follow-through at $249 per month, in US dollars. Before you pay, the workspace shows the plan, the services and limits it includes, the monthly price and these terms. Payment is processed by Stripe; you enter card details on Stripe's checkout page, and Stripe's terms apply to the payment.

  • Your subscription starts when checkout completes. There is no free trial on paid plans.
  • You are charged the monthly price at the start of each monthly period, and the subscription renews automatically every month until you end renewal.
  • The monthly price shown at checkout is the full monthly charge for the plan. We do not add usage overage charges to it.
  • Some services need third-party resources, such as a dedicated phone number or carrier registration. Their one-time and monthly costs are shown in your workspace, and you accept them separately before we obtain them.
  • If a payment fails, we may pause paid services until it is paid.
  • We may change prices. We will email you at least 30 days before a new price applies to your subscription, and you can end renewal before then.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period; your services continue until then, and the workspace shows the date renewal ends. No further charges are made after that date.

We do not give refunds or credits for partial months or unused time, except where the law requires it. Ending renewal does not request a refund. Fees paid to third parties for numbers and carrier registration are not refundable. After cancellation you can return by choosing a plan and paying again.

We do not guarantee bookings, quotes, conversions, or that any text, call or email will be delivered. Your customers are told they are talking to an automated front desk; do not present it as a person.
