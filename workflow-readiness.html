A useful first test needs a clear owner, representative information and a result people can check. Use these questions to decide what to gather next.

Readiness signals

  • Clear owner and approval boundary
  • Current handling time, errors and workload are understood
  • Defined data sources and permissions
  • Known exception types
  • Measurable success criteria

Risk checks

  • Is there a human approval gate for high-impact actions?
  • Can the responsible person review each important system action?
  • Do you have a rollback plan?

Next step

  • Turn each unanswered question into a next step: gather a sample, name a reviewer or define an acceptable result.
  • Bring those questions to a focused conversation about the task.
