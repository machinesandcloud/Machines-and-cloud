What yield forecasting could help with

A forecast is useful when planners can see its uncertainty and understand what changed. Compare predictions made at the same point in the season, then review how errors would affect storage, staffing and commitments.

  • Who reviews it: Farm management and commercial planning
  • What to measure: Forecast accuracy, planning quality, revenue confidence
  • Example test: Field-level forecast dashboard with confidence ranges and driver explanations.
