What route and dispatch optimization could help with

A workable route must reflect driver limits, vehicle capacity and customer commitments. Review the proposed changes with dispatchers and compare them with what happened on actual runs.

  • Who reviews it: Transportation operations
  • What to measure: Miles, fuel, on-time delivery, utilization
  • Example test: Dispatcher advisory dashboard with route recommendations, reasons, and manager override.
