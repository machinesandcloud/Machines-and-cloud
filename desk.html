Texting terms your customers can read: 

        
Commands you can text back
Every lead text carries a ref. Reply ok REF to acknowledge, called REF when you have reached someone who asked for a call, otw REF to send the on-the-way text, done REF when the job is finished (starts the review request), invoice REF 450 if they still owe you (payment reminders on day 3 and 7), paid REF to stop them, quote REF 1450 when you sent a quote (day-14 check), cancel REF. Follow-up texts only go out between 9 am and 9 pm (noon to 9 pm on Sunday).
