Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Machines & Cloud collects through this website and your customer workspace, how we use it, and how to contact us about privacy questions.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit through forms, scheduling links, or direct email
  • Business information you share when discussing workflows, requirements, or project scope
  • Basic usage and analytics information collected through site measurement tools, if enabled

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery or follow-up conversations
  • Evaluate potential project fit, workflow scope, and service requirements
  • Improve site content, performance, and business communications

Signup and email preferences

When you start setup, we save the contact and business details you submit so you can continue onboarding and we can respond to your request. Verifying your email protects access to your workspace. It does not, by itself, subscribe you to marketing.

Email updates and offers are optional. Your initial preference is saved with your inquiry; you can confirm or decline it after signing in. We keep a record of the preference and confirmation. You can change your email preference during signed-in setup. This email choice does not give permission for marketing texts or calls.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers involved in site hosting, scheduling, analytics, communications, or project delivery when that sharing is necessary to operate the business.

Google Calendar workspace connection

What you allow and how we use it

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Google asks for calendar details and access to events on calendars you own. This workspace connection reads your primary calendar’s identifier and creates, checks, updates or deletes the visit events it manages when a saved visit changes. It does not import your other appointments or use Google busy-time data to set your availability.

A synced event can contain the customer’s name, phone number, job address, issue, notes, urgency, visit date and arrival window. Those facts come from separate workspace records supplied by you or the customer. Google receives them when the visit is synced. Customer conversations and visit details may be processed by our AI provider to run the front desk. This Calendar connection does not send Google API responses or OAuth tokens to that provider.

Storage, sharing and retention

Our hosting provider, Netlify, stores the calendar identifier, an encrypted refresh token, connection history and sync records. Sync records include copied visit details and event references. The refresh token is sealed by the application; connection and sync records are not all encrypted by that token-protection mechanism. Connection and sync records have no automatic deletion schedule. Expiring or disconnecting a permission does not erase retained records.

We use Google API information to provide this Calendar feature and share it with service providers only as needed to operate and protect it, subject to Google’s Limited Use requirements. We do not sell it or use it for advertising or training general-purpose AI models. Human access is limited to the cases Google permits, such as your specific agreement for support, security investigations or legal obligations. Our use and transfer of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and requesting deletion

Use Disconnect Calendar in your workspace to remove the stored refresh token and stop new sync requests. A request already sent may finish. Disconnecting does not remove Google’s permission, existing Google events, saved workspace visits or sync history. You can separately remove the app’s access in your Google Account. Removing Google access also does not delete records already stored in the workspace.

For help with retained connection or sync records, email ngoumnaisteve@gmail.com and identify your workspace. Tell us whether you want help with app-held records, Google events or both. Do not email passwords, authorization codes or tokens. We will review the request, verify your authority and explain applicable retention or legal limits; this is a support request, not an automatic deletion control. Deleting a Google event does not erase the separate workspace visit record.

Retention and security

We retain business inquiry and communication records for as long as needed to manage relationships, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information involved.

Your choices

You can contact us to request updates or deletion of information you previously shared, subject to legal and contractual obligations. You can also choose not to submit optional information through the website.
