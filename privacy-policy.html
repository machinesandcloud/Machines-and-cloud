Privacy Policy

How Machines & Cloud collects, uses, shares and keeps information when you visit this website, use your customer workspace, or contact a service business that uses our AI front desk.

Who we are and our role

Machines And Cloud LLC (Machines & Cloud) is a Houston, Texas company. We provide software that answers website chats, texts and phone calls for home-service businesses, saves visit requests and leads, and keeps the business owner informed.

For information about business owners who use our workspace, people who contact us, and visitors to this website, Machines & Cloud decides how the information is used. For information about a business's own customers (for example a homeowner who chats with, texts or calls a plumbing company that uses our software), we process it on that business's behalf and under its instructions, on every channel: website chat, texts, calls, Google Calendar and lead platforms. If you are a customer of one of those businesses, that business is your first point of contact for privacy requests; each business also has its own hosted policy page. We will help the business answer your request, and we act directly on texting opt-outs.

Information we collect

Business owners and workspace users

  • Name, email address, mobile number, business name, trade, service area, address, website, hours, services, prices and the rules you save for your front desk
  • Private notes and instructions you give the front desk
  • Legal business details you provide to register texting with the carriers, such as legal name, EIN, address and authorized contact. We store these encrypted.
  • Sign-in records: your email address, one-time sign-in links and session records
  • Billing records: plan, subscription status, payment status and Stripe customer and subscription identifiers. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout page and are not stored by us.
  • Support requests, your email preference for updates and offers, and records of changes made to your workspace

Customers of businesses that use our software

  • Name, phone number, job address, the issue or service requested, urgency, preferred visit date and arrival window, and notes
  • Website chat messages and the replies the front desk sends
  • Text messages to and from the business's number, delivery status and message identifiers
  • Phone calls to the business's front desk number: caller number, call times, a written transcript (up to 12,000 characters) and an AI summary. We do not record call audio.
  • Texting permission records: when a customer agreed to appointment texts, the wording they agreed to, and any STOP or START request
  • Lead details sent by Thumbtack or Angi when the business connects that lead source
  • Visit details copied to the business's Google Calendar, if the business connects it

Website visitors and people who contact us

  • Details you submit in our forms or by email: name, email, business and your message
  • Messages you type into the "Ask the front desk" chat on this website and the homepage examples
  • Texts you send to our Customer Care number, described in Text messaging
  • Technical data used to prevent abuse: IP address and the time of a request. Where we keep a counter linked to a network, we store a one-way hash of the address, except for the short-lived records described in Retention.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this website or in the workspace.

Where the information comes from

  • From you, when you sign up, set up your workspace, connect a service, pay, or contact us
  • From a business's customers, when they use website chat, text or call the business's front desk number
  • From services a business connects: Google Calendar, Thumbtack and Angi
  • From our providers, such as Stripe (payment status), Twilio (message and call delivery status) and Vapi (call transcripts and summaries)
  • From your browser or device, such as IP address and request times

How we use information

  • Run the front desk for a business: answer customers in chat, by text and on calls, check availability, save visit requests and leads, and send the business owner email notifications
  • Send appointment confirmations, reminders and cancellation texts for a business, only to customers who agreed to them in writing
  • Sync visits to Google Calendar and receive leads from Thumbtack or Angi when the business connects them
  • Set up and register a business's phone number and texting program with carriers
  • Sign you in, keep your account secure, process payments and manage your subscription
  • Answer support questions, including texts to our Customer Care number
  • Send you email updates and offers, only if you confirmed that preference
  • Prevent abuse and fraud, enforce usage limits, keep the service working, and meet legal obligations

We do not use a business's customer information to train AI models, to run another business's front desk, or for our own marketing.

How AI processes conversations

The front desk writes its replies with an AI model provided by Anthropic. To answer a customer, we send Anthropic the conversation so far, the business's saved details, rules and notes, and the result of availability checks (for example, whether a time window is open). This applies to website chat, texts and calls. The "Ask the front desk" chat on this website and the homepage examples also use Anthropic. On calls, Vapi runs the conversation and Deepgram, working for Vapi, turns speech into text; Vapi returns the transcript and a summary to us.

Anthropic does not use these inputs and outputs to train its models by default, and it states that it deletes them within 30 days, unless they are flagged for a usage-policy review, in which case it can keep them longer. We do not train AI models on your data. AI replies can be wrong, so business owners review their rules, test their front desk and see each saved conversation. The front desk does not make decisions about people that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Service providers we use

These providers process information for us only as needed to run the service, each under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Netlify: website hosting, application functions and data storage
  • Anthropic: the AI model that writes front desk replies and call responses
  • Vapi (with Deepgram for transcription): answering and transcribing phone calls
  • Twilio: phone numbers, call routing, text messages and carrier registration
  • Resend: sending email, such as sign-in links and owner notifications from desk@machinesandcloud.com
  • Stripe: checkout, card payments, invoices and subscription management
  • Google: Google Calendar, only if a business connects it
  • Thumbtack and Angi: lead platforms that send us new leads, only if a business connects them

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share information only with the providers listed above to run the service; with the business whose customer you are; with a service a business has asked us to connect; when the law requires it or to protect people, our service or our rights; and with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganized, under this policy. Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data and texting consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Google Calendar workspace connection

What you allow and how we use it

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. Google asks for calendar details and access to events on calendars you own. This workspace connection reads your primary calendar’s identifier and creates, checks, updates or deletes the visit events it manages when a saved visit changes. After you review the current Calendar access details, it also reads busy intervals on that calendar before offering a new visit or moving one. The availability check requests event references, start and end times, the calendar's time zone, recurrence rules, cancellation and transparency status, self-attendance status and private event properties, which we use to recognize the events our workspace created. It does not request appointment titles, descriptions or other attendees’ identities. Existing connections made under the earlier disclosure require review and reconnection before these availability checks.

A synced event can contain the customer’s name, phone number, job address, issue, notes, urgency, visit date and arrival window. Those facts come from separate workspace records supplied by you or the customer. Google receives them when the visit is synced. Customer conversations and visit details are processed by our AI provider, Anthropic, to run the front desk. Availability derived from your Google Calendar (whether a listed time window is open) is also shared with that provider so the front desk offers only open times. This Calendar connection does not send Google API responses or OAuth tokens to that provider, and it does not send your event details.

Storage, sharing and retention

Our hosting provider, Netlify, stores the calendar identifier, an encrypted refresh token, connection history and sync records. Sync records include copied visit details and event references. The availability cache stores only event references, start and end times and workspace ownership tags, scoped to your connection and checked date range. It is reused for at most 60 seconds; new bookings and calendar changes check Google again. Cached rows remain stored until replaced or deleted, and disconnecting makes them unusable for availability. The refresh token is sealed by the application; connection and sync records are not all encrypted by that token-protection mechanism. Connection and sync records have no automatic deletion schedule; they are erased when your workspace is erased at your request. Expiring or disconnecting a permission does not erase retained records.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve this user-facing Calendar feature, and share it with service providers only as needed to operate and protect it. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to create, train or improve any AI or machine-learning model, including general-purpose AI models. Human access is limited to the cases Google permits, such as your specific agreement for support, security investigations or legal obligations. Machines & Cloud's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and requesting deletion

Use Disconnect Calendar in your workspace to remove the stored refresh token and stop new sync requests. A request already sent may finish. Disconnecting does not remove Google’s permission, existing Google events, saved workspace visits or sync history. You can separately remove the app’s access in your Google Account. Removing Google access also does not delete records already stored in the workspace.

For help with retained connection or sync records, email support@machinesandcloud.com and identify your workspace. Tell us whether you want help with app-held records, Google events or both. Do not email passwords, authorization codes or tokens. We will review the request, verify your authority and explain applicable retention or legal limits; this is a support request, not an automatic deletion control. Deleting a Google event does not erase the separate workspace visit record, and erasing workspace records does not delete events already in your Google Calendar.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as the purpose below requires:

  • Workspace records (business details, rules, conversations, transcripts and summaries, visits, leads, texting and Calendar sync records): while your account exists, including after you stop service, so you can download them or reopen. We erase them when the business owner asks us to, as described in Your choices and rights.
  • Sign-in links expire after 20 minutes and sign-in sessions after 24 hours. Expired sign-in records, and setup hand-off records, are deleted about 7 days after they expire.
  • Short-term abuse-prevention records that contain an IP address are deleted within about 20 minutes. Daily usage counters store only a one-way hash of the network address.
  • Inquiries and unfinished setups are kept until you ask us to delete them, so you can continue where you left off.
  • Opt-out records: to keep honoring a STOP, we keep the phone number and the fact that it opted out, even after a deletion request. We remove the message content and other details.
  • Billing and payment records are kept for tax, accounting and legal purposes and are not removed by an erasure request. Stripe keeps its own payment records.
  • Copies held by providers follow their own retention: for example Anthropic (generally up to 30 days), Twilio and Vapi message and call logs, Stripe payment records, and events already in a Google Calendar.

We protect information with access controls, signed sign-in links instead of passwords, separate operator access, encryption of sensitive items such as Calendar refresh tokens, Thumbtack access tokens and carrier registration details, and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects information we hold for a business, we will notify that business promptly so it can meet its own obligations, and we will give any notice the law requires.

Browser storage and cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The workspace keeps your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in, and website chat keeps the current conversation in the browser tab so it survives a page reload. The homepage examples may remember which example you watched. You can clear this storage in your browser settings at any time.

Your choices and rights

Business owners can view and update workspace details at any time, download bookings, leads, conversations and customers as CSV files, disconnect Calendar and lead sources, pause or stop service, and end renewal. To ask for a copy, correction or erasure of your workspace, email support@machinesandcloud.com from your account email address. We confirm the request with you by email before acting. Erasure follows the end of service and removes your workspace records, sign-in records, drafts and connected-service credentials, with the exceptions listed in Retention. Phone numbers and carrier registrations we set up for you are released or closed first, which can take some time.

Customers of a business should send access, correction or deletion requests to that business. If you write to us instead, we will pass the request to the business and act on its instruction. Before we delete a customer's records, we confirm the request with the business or confirm the requester controls the phone number involved. Erasing a customer's records removes or redacts their conversations, transcripts, visits, leads and texting records in that business's workspace, but keeps the opt-out record described above. To stop texts, reply STOP at any time; we honor that directly.

Everyone can ask what personal information we hold about them, and ask us to correct or delete it. We answer within 45 days. If we decline, we explain why, and you can appeal by replying to our answer; we decide appeals within 60 days. We do not treat you differently for making a request. You can choose not to submit optional information through the website.
