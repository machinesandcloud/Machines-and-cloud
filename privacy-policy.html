Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Machines & Cloud collects through this website, how we use it, and how to contact us about privacy questions.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you submit through forms, scheduling links, or direct email
  • Business information you share when discussing workflows, requirements, or project scope
  • Basic usage and analytics information collected through site measurement tools, if enabled

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery or follow-up conversations
  • Evaluate potential project fit, workflow scope, and service requirements
  • Improve site content, performance, and business communications

Signup and email preferences

When you start setup, we save the contact and business details you submit so you can continue onboarding and we can respond to your request. Verifying your email protects access to your workspace. It does not, by itself, subscribe you to marketing.

Email updates and offers are optional. Your initial preference is saved with your inquiry; you can confirm or decline it after signing in. We keep a record of the preference and confirmation. You can change your email preference during signed-in setup. This email choice does not give permission for marketing texts or calls.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers involved in site hosting, scheduling, analytics, communications, or project delivery when that sharing is necessary to operate the business.

Text messaging

Machines And Cloud LLC (Machines & Cloud) is preparing its own Customer Care text program at (346) 485-3328. Awaiting carrier approval. Text replies are not live yet. Once approved and enabled, a person can start by texting that number about your workspace setup, account access or requested plan information. We use the inbound message as permission for direct answers about that request, not for recurring engagement. Calling, emailing, signup, booking or entering a phone number does not opt anyone into texts. This program sends no proactive updates, marketing, automatic missed-call messages or messages for shops and their homeowners.

We keep the sender's number, message content, provider message identifiers and timestamps, and the record of opt-out or restart requests. We use them to answer the inquiry, maintain a conversation record, avoid duplicate replies, prevent abuse and honor texting choices. Our hosting and messaging providers process information as necessary to provide and protect this service. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS consent is not sold, rented or transferred to another sender or program.

Message frequency varies with the messages you send; there are no scheduled or recurring messages. Message and data rates may apply. Once live, HELP, INFO or AYUDA provides our support email and account/setup links. STOP, ALTO, PARAR or another standard opt-out keyword stops texts. START, UNSTOP or EMPEZAR only removes a block; it does not subscribe you to recurring messages. We retain the records needed to honor an opt-out and handle support, security and legal obligations; a deletion request does not erase the suppression needed to prevent unwanted texts. For SMS support or SMS privacy questions, email ngoumnaisteve@gmail.com. Full SMS Terms & Conditions explain the program and all supported keywords.

Service businesses using our software have separate customer numbers, brands, campaigns and terms. Their homeowner messages and consent belong to their own program, not the Machines & Cloud Customer Care number. Under the supported US-EIN shop program, a homeowner's inbound text permits direct replies. Ongoing confirmations, reminders and cancellation updates, including future appointments with that shop, require a separate written YES or SÍ after the named business clearly asks for that ongoing permission in the conversation. Giving a number, calling or booking alone is insufficient. An earlier agreement limited to one visit is not treated as ongoing permission. We process the shop's message and versioned consent records on its behalf; they are not used for another shop's marketing. Each shop's hosted policy describes its program, including any distinct legacy arrangement.

Retention and security

We retain business inquiry and communication records for as long as needed to manage relationships, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information involved.

Your choices

You can contact us to request updates or deletion of information you previously shared, subject to legal and contractual obligations. You can also choose not to submit optional information through the website.
