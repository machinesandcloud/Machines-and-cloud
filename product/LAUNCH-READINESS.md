## Competitor lessons translated into acceptance

- **Test before forwarding the real line.** Goodcall documents choosing an agent number, testing it and then forwarding the business line; it also separates team access from transfer contacts. Our acceptance should prove a real test call and handoff before routing customers, while keeping operator access separate from an escalation destination. [Goodcall FAQ](https://www.goodcall.com/faq).
- **Ask about decisions, not internal infrastructure.** Jobber's setup presents greeting, permitted automatic actions, escalation contacts, business knowledge and a test. Its request outcomes and assigned follow-up tasks connect conversations to work. Our one journey should collect those owner decisions and prepare the provider resources underneath it. [Jobber Receptionist documentation](https://getjobber.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/25315927533847-Receptionist-powered-by-Jobber-AI).
- **Reuse saved settings and show where the result arrives.** Housecall Pro documents using existing business/booking/service-area settings, while acknowledging carrier forwarding and specialist assistance. Its chat documentation makes the website widget a prerequisite and puts outcomes in the inbox. Our onboarding should reuse verified answers, expose the remaining permission/installation step and prove the resulting request in the portal. [Housecall Pro setup](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12996637-getting-started-with-csr-ai), [chat workflow](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12453258-csr-ai-chat-answering).
