## What the first shop must be able to do

A home-service owner supplies business information once, sees a usable draft, corrects it without losing work, understands what the front desk will say and tries it. The platform carries the information into each requested connection, runs permitted setup automatically, and shows what is waiting on the owner, carrier or provider. After activation, a real inquiry produces an accurate saved request or visit and a usable owner handoff. The owner can find it, take responsibility, change a visit, pause service and recover from a failure.

The owner still makes their own account, permission, carrier and cost decisions. Automation handles the resulting setup and recovery; the founder should not copy credentials between systems or finish routine customer configuration. Entering a website URL does not authorize changing that website. A website administrator must grant access or install the widget through a supported path.

The PO accepts that complete outcome for the agreed channel scope. A narrower free website pilot can be considered explicitly with its participant; it does not count as delivery of the complete advertised offer. Billing remains parked. Paid activation requires a separately agreed, working service and settled usage/overage terms rather than charging for unavailable channels.

## Competitor lessons translated into acceptance

The following official documentation was read for this review. It describes vendor-published behavior, not competitor products we tested or customer interviews.

- **Test before forwarding the real line.** Goodcall documents choosing an agent number, testing it and then forwarding the business line; it also separates team access from transfer contacts. Our acceptance should prove a real test call and handoff before routing customers, while keeping operator access separate from an escalation destination. [Goodcall FAQ](https://www.goodcall.com/faq).
- **Ask about decisions, not internal infrastructure.** Jobber's setup presents greeting, permitted automatic actions, escalation contacts, business knowledge and a test. Its request outcomes and assigned follow-up tasks connect conversations to work. Our one journey should collect those owner decisions and prepare the provider resources underneath it. [Jobber Receptionist documentation](https://getjobber.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/25315927533847-Receptionist-powered-by-Jobber-AI).
- **Reuse saved settings and show where the result arrives.** Housecall Pro documents using existing business/booking/service-area settings, while acknowledging carrier forwarding and specialist assistance. Its chat documentation makes the website widget a prerequisite and puts outcomes in the inbox. Our onboarding should reuse verified answers, expose the remaining permission/installation step and prove the resulting request in the portal. [Housecall Pro setup](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12996637-getting-started-with-csr-ai), [chat workflow](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/12453258-csr-ai-chat-answering).

These lessons support the selected implementation and acceptance work. They do not establish that our service is better than a competitor or that an unbuilt connector is available.
