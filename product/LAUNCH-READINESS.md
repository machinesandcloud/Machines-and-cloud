## Functional scope still requiring a product decision

**Google connection currently synchronizes visits outward; it does not read existing appointments or busy time.** `get_availability` explicitly checks owner blocks and front-desk reservations only (`netlify/functions/lib/availability.mjs:67`); the connection disclosure and next step repeat that limit (`desk-connections.js:217`, `netlify/functions/lib/connection-journey.mjs:38`). Work booked elsewhere can conflict unless the owner manually blocks it. This is a capability gap, not merely a missing live test. General automatic scheduling must prevent those conflicts, or the owner must explicitly accept a narrower visit-sync scope with that decision made clear before activation. We cannot claim existing-calendar conflict protection.

**Automatic dedicated texting currently supports registered US-EIN LLCs, corporations and partnerships.** The registration normalizer rejects unsupported business types (`netlify/functions/lib/messaging-activation.mjs:65–79`), and the current form offers only those types. A sole proprietor must not reach a cost decision assuming the supported registration path covers them. PO/Architect must define the eligible first cohort and show the eligibility before cost acceptance, or implement and verify an additional lawful provider registration path.

**Current AI capacity is a shared safety budget, not a paid-service entitlement.** `netlify/functions/lib/self-service-ai.mjs:11–15` defaults to 50 accepted logical turns per Houston day across all new tenants, with separate per-tenant private/public ceilings; failed or ambiguous starts consume reservations. One busy shop can exhaust capacity for another. A funded first cohort needs measured inference cost, explicit customer allowances and an approved admission/fairness policy before paid launch. Raising an environment value alone is not that acceptance.

**Customers grant authority; the platform performs the resulting work.** They choose the Google account, attest legal/consent facts, approve routing/costs and authorize or perform website installation. Those are necessary customer decisions. Number/assistant configuration, approved sender registration tracking, visit delivery, retries and recovery must then run without routine founder tenant edits. Adding a website URL alone does not authorize modifying that website. The current snippet/handoff is a real installation path, not an automatic website integration.
