# Optional specialist setup — October 22, 2026

**Decision proposed:** keep standard workspace and supported automatic setup free. Automate every supported provider operation first. Offer a specialist only for customer-controlled work that is both feasible and genuinely optional, or for separately scoped custom integration/migration. Never charge the customer to repair Machines & Cloud, wait for carrier review, or remove an availability restriction we have not solved.

This is current public pricing research and a proposed service contract, not a published offer, hired specialist, accepted quote, payment implementation or live connection. Steve authorized investigating available APIs and an optional paid specialist fallback. Billing remains parked. No provider account action, purchase, customer contact or publication is part of this review.

## Preserve the current offer

The homepage says **free to set up, no card required**, and creates no subscription or timed trial. Coverage is $99/month, Follow-through is $249/month and Enterprise is custom pricing for later paid service. Standard setup must not become a surprise implementation charge. A customer who needs help because our supported automation failed must receive platform support, not an upsell.

## Pricing recommendation

**Do not change the subscription prices or current free setup.** Prepare an optional **$499 one-time Specialist Setup** hypothesis for a single shop, but expose it only after a real specialist arrangement, supported-systems list, payment/cancellation policy and capacity have been accepted. Until then the honest action is **Request setup help**, without checkout, guaranteed scheduling or a statement that someone has been assigned.

Proposed fixed scope:

- One business location and one existing phone line, plus installation on one compatible website if authorized access is available and the combined engagement fits the stated limit.
- Up to two hours of configuration/session time and a short written or recorded handover; one bounded correction/retest for that agreed work.
- Existing business information reused from the workspace; no repeated long questionnaire and no request to email credentials.
- Eligibility and a written checklist before payment. If the scope does not fit, offer free guided steps or a separate quote before starting. Failed eligibility or an unavailable specialist does not require a purchase.
- Deliver the agreed configuration with specific verification receipts, rollback instructions and a customer-visible result. **Connected** still requires the platform's own current delivery evidence; a specialist's “done” note is insufficient.

The $499 hypothesis is at the upper end of the directly advertised $299–$499 configured-service setup fees. Those competitors also earn recurring subscriptions; the comparison does not validate our price or labor cost. It is **our recommendation**, not an industry average, measured willingness-to-pay result or approved public price. Its margin must be checked against actual fulfillment. Illustrative cost test only: two service hours plus a half-hour handover/QA at a hypothetical loaded $125/hour, plus $25 reserve, costs $337.50; $499 leaves $161.50 (32.4%) before fixed overhead/tax. A specialist charging $500 wholesale makes a $499 retail offer unviable. Contract a workable cost or quote that job separately; don't silently reduce the customer's promised scope.

For genuine custom integration/migration, quote after feasibility rather than publish a flat fee. The observed $1,500–$5,000 build prices are reference points with broader scopes, not an automatic quote. Use estimated labor, independent testing, vendor charges and maintenance obligations to price our actual scope. Customer-approved platform/usage/carrier fees remain distinct from specialist labor. An external specialist referral may use that specialist's own written contract; disclose who delivers, who charges and who handles remediation.

## Customer flow and state contract

This is a proposed implementation contract; it is not present merely because this document exists.

1. **Identify the exact remaining action.** Derive a reason from current connection receipts and eligibility, not from a marketing preference. Continue background work that is still possible.
2. **Show the free path first.** A short supported action, expected result and Verify button. Offer a specialist alongside it only for eligible external configuration; never make payment the default continuation.
3. **Preflight optional help.** Reuse business, carrier and host facts. Ask only missing system/version/admin-access information, desired result and availability/time zone. Do not collect passwords, MFA, card details or full legal input in a generic help request.
4. **Present a written scope.** Named fulfillment organization, exact one-time total, separately identified third-party costs, deliverables, exclusions, required owner participation, cancellation/refund terms and actual availability. No fee acceptance by merely clicking a general onboarding CTA.
5. **Assign without Steve.** A staffed provider queue claims the exact-tenant job; automated scheduling, reminders and task handoff follow only where separately authorized/implemented. A temporarily unavailable provider produces a truthful availability state, not a founder callback promise.
6. **Grant limited access and configure.** Explicit delegated permission, minimum allowed actions, expiry, audit and revocation; never operator-wide or other-customer access. Owner personally completes account login/MFA, OAuth and attestations.
7. **Verify and hand back.** Run the specific authorized tests, store evidence, show what is working and what is waiting, deliver the handover and revoke temporary access. Record one accepted result, not an inferred success from a completed calendar appointment.

| State / classification | Visible explanation and next action | Advancement rule |
|---|---|---|
| `automatic_in_progress` | “We're preparing your connection. You can leave and come back.” | Current worker receipts only; safe retry/cancel supported. |
| `needs_owner_action` | One exact task, such as Connect Google or Forward missed calls; free instructions. | Provider return or verified customer action/test, with current tenant/revision fences. |
| `waiting_provider` | Explain carrier/provider review and continue polling. | Provider result; no paid bypass. |
| `needs_platform_fix` | “This connection needs our attention.” Preserve setup; free support incident. | Platform repair and fresh verification; no specialist upsell. |
| `specialist_eligible` | “Prefer help with this step?” Show scope preview and Request setup help. | Eligibility, actual fulfillment availability and an owner request. |
| `specialist_requested` / `quoted` | Request retained; exact scope/total or awaiting confirmed availability. | Accepted current quote, with explicit payment authorization only if billing is implemented. Request alone charges nothing. |
| `scheduled` / `in_progress` | Specialist identity, appointment/task status, scope and access-revocation action. | Provider assignment and bounded delegated action receipts. |
| `waiting_owner` / `waiting_external` | Specific blocker with one action or a truthful wait state. | Required owner permission/provider decision; no silent extra charges. |
| `verifying` / `completed` | Show tested outcomes and remaining unavailable connections separately. | Current independent platform proof for each connection; then revoke temporary access and close only the accepted scope. |
| `cancelled` / `not_available` | What stopped, any required rollback/refund result, and free/supported alternative. | Preserve audit and uncertain effects; never silently restart the paid job. |

Human help must not create a second untracked provisioning engine. Requests, scopes, quote versions, authorizations, assignments and verification refer to the existing workspace/connection operations. Idempotency prevents duplicate paid jobs and repeated provider changes; revision/lifecycle checks prevent stale configuration. Customer sign-out, grant revocation and cancellation are distinct recorded events. Access to another shop or a specialist completion flag cannot satisfy service readiness.

- OM has a real contracted fulfillment route, pricing/capacity and escalation ownership that do not depend on Steve. No message or appointment is sent merely to test this proposal.
