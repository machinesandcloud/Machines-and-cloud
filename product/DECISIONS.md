# Decisions log

Engineer notes on trade-offs made while building. Newest first.

- 2026-10-20, round 4 items 4 and 5 built ahead of the sprint 4 cut. (4) Every booking schedules a `visit_check` task at 9 am Houston the day after its window; if the booking is still booked, confirmed, or on the way, the owner gets one "Did the visit ... happen? Reply done REF or cancel REF" text (`visitCheckedAt` prevents a repeat; `done`, `cancel`, and reschedule clear or move the task). (5) Spanish stop words (ALTO, PARAR, CANCELAR, BASTA, DETENER, "no me manden más mensajes", "no más mensajes", "dejen de escribir") opt out like STOP and get a Spanish confirmation; EMPEZAR, INICIAR, and SÍ opt back in when the number is out; the Spanish confirmation and consent line say "STOP o ALTO"; a repeat STOP from a number already out is silent (one confirmation only). Steve adds ALTO and PARAR as custom opt-out keywords on the Messaging Service (OPERATIONS.md one-time setup).

- 2026-10-20, round 4 MUSTs built ahead of the sprint 4 cut. (1) Booking dates are stored as `YYYY-MM-DD` in Houston time (`normalizeDate` at booking and reschedule; the customer's words are kept in `dateSaid`); records written earlier with "today" or "tomorrow" are read relative to their `createdAt` (`bookingDay`), so no migration pass is needed; customer-facing texts render the date with `friendlyDate` (today, tomorrow, "Sat, Oct 24"; Spanish hoy, mañana, long form). `callerContext`, `upcomingBookings`, and `customerType` take an `at` for tests (`POST /api/vapi` body `at`). (2) `done REF` on a Growth or Pro shop with no review link schedules no review request, nudge, or referral ask; the reply names the portal field and `review_skipped` is logged; the checklist has the line; the build keeps a Google review URL the crawl finds. (3) `parseHours` ignores a "24/7" phrase that comes with "emergency", "on call", or "after hours", or after day ranges; such a phrase sets `rules.emergency247` (from onboarding, the hours field on the portal, or an explicit PUT), and the prompt then handles a Tier 1 after hours as Tier 0 with the after-hours fee stated. When the shop is closed but opens later the same day, the callback promise reads "when we open at 8 this morning" and `callbackDueAt` is that opening minute (`opensLaterToday`).

- 2026-10-20, sprint 3 (S3-05 to S3-10). Emergency table: `rules.emergencies` is `{ tier0, tier1, tier2, script }` prefilled by `tradeKey(trade)`; old string values are read as the script, so no migration. Tier 1 opens a `today` lead, not the emergency chain, unless a vulnerable person is in the home (PM assumption 6). Structured hours: `rules.hours` per weekday, parsed from the hours sentence by `parseHours` (handles "Mon to Fri 7 to 6, Sat 8 to 2", am/pm, "closed", "24/7"; bare hours under 5 are read as pm for opens and under 10 as pm for closes); the 7 am to 7 pm fallback applies when nothing parsed, and the portal says so. The prompt now states the Houston time and open/closed, which is what makes the after-hours sentence testable (`admin prompt ... at`). Callback promises: `callbackDue` parses "within N minutes", "within the hour", "by H am/pm" (next occurrence), "tomorrow"; unknown phrases default to one hour. Weekly email: "actionable conversation" is any conversation with a customer message in the window that is not spam and not from a bypass number; the booking rate divides bookings by that count; `nobook_reason` is a required enum on `notify_owner` (strict tools need every field, so "none" exists). Spanish: fixed template strings drafted by the model on 2026-10-20, review by a Spanish speaker named by Steve pending; owner-facing texts stay English. Customers export: `Lead Source` is always "Other" because Jobber accepts seven fixed values; `Tags` is "front desk"; addresses without a ZIP take the shop's city. Acknowledging an alert now cancels its pending `escalate` task.

- 2026-10-20, round 3 MUSTs built ahead of the sprint 3 cut (PO items 1 to 3; the PM writes the tickets after the fact). Quiet hours: `scheduleFollowUp` in `lib/desk.mjs` moves review requests, nudges, quote checks, lead chases, referral asks, and payment reminders to the next minute inside 9 am to 9 pm Houston (noon to 9 pm Sunday), the Texas ch. 304 hours (PO question 1 assumed). Visit reminders, confirmations, on-the-way, text-backs, and live replies are not deferred. Payment reminders are no longer scheduled by `done`; `invoice REF amount` schedules day 3 and 7 and `paid REF` cancels them. The referral ask is gated: the review request is recorded on the booking (`reviewAskedAt`) and in the conversation; the first customer text after it is classified by a 5-token Claude call (positive, negative, none) in `noteReviewReply`; negative cancels the nudge and the ask and texts the owner NEEDS ACTION; positive cancels the nudge; the scheduler sends `referral_ask` only when the reply was positive and `review_nudge` only when there was no reply. Every scheduled follow-up is now also written into the SMS conversation (`system: <type>`) so the front desk knows what it sent when the customer answers.
- 2026-10-20, S2-14. A booking closes the customer's open leads from the last 24 hours (status `booked`, alerts acknowledged by "booking"); the merge branch of `flagLead` recomputes new or existing.

- 2026-10-19, sprint 2. Netlify Blobs stores are opened per call, not cached at module load: a warm function instance that outlived the Blobs token failed every write with "Token expired" (seen on the draft after about an hour of traffic). Production functions kept warm by the scheduler would have hit the same wall.
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2 (S2-07). The Twilio number is imported into Vapi without a fixed `assistantId`; Vapi sends `assistant-request` to `/api/vapi` on every call and we answer with the assistant id plus `assistantOverrides` (first message and `variableValues` for the caller's name, existing-customer flag, upcoming visit, VIP flag). Trade-off: if our webhook does not answer within Vapi's timeout the call gets Vapi's error, where a fixed assistant would still have answered; accepted because the same webhook already runs every tool call. Shape written from the Vapi docs as of 2026-10; confirmed in the keyed pass (S2-01).
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2 (S2-08). A VIP voicemail is a Twilio `<Record>` posted to `/api/voice/recorded`; the owner gets the recording link by text. On a one-phone shop (owner's cell is the forwarded line) no `<Dial>` is attempted, because the forwarded cell would bounce the call back into the front desk. On a Vapi-answered one-phone shop the assistant takes the message with a "leave a message" greeting and `vip: yes` in the prompt variables.
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2 (S2-09). Texts are counted on send success (or on the logged path only when Twilio is not configured) by type: customer-facing types only. Minutes are `ceil(seconds / 60)` per call from `end-of-call-report`; a missed call that got the text-back counts 0 minutes. The overage line on the portal card is the one price outside the homepage (PM assumption 9), for Steve's veto.
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2. Web and test-line chat now use `dropToolPreamble` like SMS: a turn that calls a tool shows the customer only the text written after the tool returned, because the model's planning line ("Customer asked for a person; call notify_owner") was reaching the website chat. The final turn still streams word by word; only the pre-tool fragment is held back.
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2 (S2-11). Rate limit is 60 chat messages per IP per 10 minutes, counted in a Blobs record keyed by IP and 10-minute bucket (two Blobs operations per message; old buckets are never read again). The owner test line with a valid key and the admin key are exempt.
- 2026-10-19, sprint 2 (S2-12). Number release on a cancelled shop clears the number index and the record's number fields even without Twilio or Vapi keys (`number_release_skipped`), so the old number cannot route; the keys release the number and assistant for real (`number_released`). The old number is kept as `phone.releasedNumber`.
