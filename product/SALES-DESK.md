## The owner experience

- Answer the question first. Use known trade, area, team, rules and concern; ask at most one unanswered question when it changes the advice. Zero questions is often appropriate.
- Do useful work before asking for contact details: a missed-call reply, three intake/handoff rules, a website FAQ, or a short implementation/fit plan. Use the owner's facts; leave unknowns unknown. A draft is never described as sent or installed.
- Keep the thread straight. Corrections replace stale values and dependent calculations. Do not infer missed calls from total calls, profit from revenue, or customer demand from team size. Explain necessary assumptions and arithmetic.
- Let the owner disagree, decline or leave. Recommend waiting, a simpler process, a person or a native competing workflow when that fits better. No compulsory email, repeated CTA, invented urgency or rebuttal loop.
- Speak the visitor's language naturally. Preserve names, locations, prices and corrections. If asked, produce an English owner brief alongside Spanish customer wording; do not infer ethnicity or claim validated Spanish voice.
- Be concise without sounding clipped: a direct price question needs a short answer; a requested plan deserves enough detail to use. Avoid canned praise, a brochure repeated every turn, and a question tacked onto every answer.
- Offer setup, the relevant practice example or contact as choices the visitor clicks. No automatic navigation, signup, sending, booking or claim someone was notified. Do not expose commits, test fixtures or storage jargon in buyer copy; explain the practical availability limit.

Facts must match the release actually bundled. Before publication, do not tell owners the new workspace is already live. When the accepted workspace is released, describe email verification and review/test gates accurately; do not carry an “unpublished” statement forever or imply phone/SMS readiness from the website-chat release. Published prices are plan preferences while billing is off. Use no numerical market claim without a reviewed primary source, date, population and context. Owner-provided arithmetic is an estimate, never promised recovery or ROI.

## Quality gate and proof stages

PO scores each applicable conversation from 0–3 on **direct answer/diagnosis, usable value, context/corrections, natural pacing, calibrated truth and visitor control**. Zero means failed/harmful, 1 generic/incomplete, 2 useful with a material weakness, 3 specific and appropriate. Passing bar: **15/18 per thread, truth and control both 3, no dimension 0**. Do not penalize a short price answer for omitting an unrequested essay or a question. Measure the result, not adjectives or regex matches. Spanish semantic review is explicit; named human Spanish approval remains separate.

Any invented proof/statistic, unsupported current capability, stale corrected fact, false send/callback, forced capture, secret disclosure or unreliable completion is a hard return regardless of score. The [independent retained baseline](SALES-DESK-QA.md) is a defect register, not evidence that this new quality bar passes; repeated assertions of one failure are not counted as separate defects, and the focus-timing collector return is kept distinct.

**Can be accepted offline:** fact/tool contracts, deterministic calculations, no auto-capture/action, source/mode isolation, model-attempt limits, widget accessibility, long-thread/retry/reset/stream behavior and rendered actions. QA supplies independent evidence; PO reviews actual screens and outputs.

**Requires separate authorization:** actual-model multi-turn quality, natural Spanish, relevant uncertainty and generated work-product judgment. Fixtures cannot establish that it is exceptionally intelligent. Root must define a new bounded request ledger counting actual HTTP attempts/retries before any rehearsal; earlier budgets stay closed. Actual-model acceptance still does not prove hosted behavior, provider delivery or customer adoption. Publication requires its own concrete approval.
