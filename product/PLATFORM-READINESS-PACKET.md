## 1. Customer outcome and paid provisioning eligibility

One verified signup saves the shop's services, area, hours and booking rules. In one Connections journey the customer authorizes their number and usage, attests to their own legal/consent facts, and connects Google. Workers perform ordinary provisioning and recovery; the portal asks for a customer action only when an external requirement needs it. The admin supervises exceptions. Steve does not approve or configure every shop.

The current automatic texting adapter supports a **private US LLC, corporation or partnership with a nine-digit EIN**, an address in one of the 50 states or DC, a public HTTPS business website, and an authorized representative with a working email, `+1` phone and supported position. Legal name/EIN/address must match the actual registered business. The platform provisions a dedicated local voice/SMS number first; the texting adapter then creates that shop's Standard Brand, CUSTOMER_CARE campaign and Messaging Service. This is the implementation's narrower supported path, not all the businesses Twilio can register. Stage A is an internal free-workspace smoke and requires none of these carrier filings. No sole-proprietor/no-EIN path, US territories, public companies, nonprofits, special campaigns, porting, multi-location/FSM integration or international delivery. An unsupported customer keeps their saved workspace and sees the relevant unavailable connection; the platform must not invent legal fields or force them through another brand type. Calendar currently supports the owner's primary calendar and outbound saved-visit create/change/cancel only. External busy-time import and two-way appointment synchronization are not included.
