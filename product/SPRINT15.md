## Delivered locally

- The setup page now has a dedicated responsive design, one active progress stage, grouped optional details, readable customer language and the existing accessible error/focus behavior. New version-2 creation requires trade, services, service area, usable hours and arrival windows; incomplete drafts still save. Optional prices, FAQs and advanced policies remain optional.
- Phone selection collects the existing business line/carrier and missed/all-call choice, or the preference to use a new number. A separate contact cell prevents forwarding loops. The selected mode survives authenticated draft saves and is carried into the owned workspace. Completion points to **Review my front desk** in that workspace.
- The Connections area directs unreviewed owners to their saved business answers and untested owners to a private rehearsal. Eligible owners then review one concrete number/voice/text offer with the applicable legal, representative, cost and consent statements. Closed platform gates expose saved preferences without collecting financial consent or registration data.
- `service-onboarding.mjs` coordinates the existing engines rather than duplicating provider logic. It persists encrypted admission and one acceptance, requests a durable number intent, then requests the selected voice/text children after the number receipt. Actual dedicated worker/scheduler hooks recover work without keeping the customer's browser open.
- Tenant, current owner, required revision, lifecycle, exact offer/program digests, credentials and child identities are checked before continuation. Safe pre-dispatch changes can be reviewed again; uncertain invoked outcomes retain their receipts. Cancellation propagates to recorded children and number setup, including closed-gate cancellation, without replacing identities. A reserved number or queued child never means active service.
- The owner sees current progress and the next relevant review, correction, forwarding or delivery check. Health tracks missing receipts, failed/stale automatic work and customer-action states. Legal fields are removed from the connection dialog after Back, native close or success.

Final review returned a worker fairness mismatch: one candidate every ten minutes could not revisit the retained cohort within the 25-minute health window. Recovery now runs every two minutes, visits at most five distinct candidates under one shared 12-second deadline and emits sanitized progress. A separate actual worker/health case confirms an expected owner review stays visible without reporting a global outage, while missing/uncertain receipts remain failures. These are bounded local scheduling results, not a live throughput or maximum activation-time guarantee; in-flight storage work is not forcibly interrupted by the shared deadline.
