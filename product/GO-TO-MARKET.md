# Marketing, digital assets and sales

Owner: Marketing / Digital Assets / Sales. Established October 22, 2026.

This role is accountable for helping the right service-business owners discover the product, understand its value, try it and become successful customers. It owns positioning, acquisition copy, lead qualification, consent-aware audience records, useful sales materials and the measurement of that journey. Its work continues after a page is written.

The first audience remains small HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning and related service businesses. Use “business” when speaking across trades; do not invent a dealership segment or claim that all owners share the same problem. Operator research in `../RESEARCH-stakeholder-forums.md` informs hypotheses; customer interviews and observed use must establish which ones matter to actual buyers.

## Operating responsibilities

| Work | Deliverable and acceptance |
|---|---|
| Positioning and copy | Describe the customer's job and a useful result in plain language. Verify every product, price, integration, timing and savings claim against current behavior. No invented testimonials, customer logos, conversion gains or “instant” activation. |
| Acquisition | Give visitors a short first step, capture the details they choose to submit, and carry those answers into one onboarding journey. Measure successful saves and continuation, not merely button clicks. |
| Qualification | Use the visitor's stated goal to choose a relevant example and next question. Do not turn expressed interest in phone, texting or calendars into a claim that the service is active. |
| Digital assets | Maintain the homepage entry, goal-specific examples, concise product explanations and relevant help/tutorial assets with UI/UX and the PO. Preserve source and customer evidence behind claims. |
| Email audience | Keep anonymous contact capture, verified account ownership, requested marketing preference, confirmed marketing preference and suppression separate. A lead row or a click on Continue does not make someone an email subscriber. |
| Sales assistance | Help visitors assess fit and prepare useful next steps. The AI adviser must respect corrections, say when a required workflow is unavailable and let visitors proceed without a sales call. |
| Measurement | Report eligible audience size, form completion, verification, setup completion, first useful test, activation and retention with denominators. Keep synthetic/local checks separate from real customer results. |

Marketing owns customer-facing language; the PO owns the selected customer outcome; UI/UX owns interaction clarity; the Architect owns identity/consent/state contracts; Engineering owns implementation; QA independently tests the journey; OM owns sender and operational readiness. A landing-page change cannot close an activation dependency or establish delivery.

## Current acquisition decision

The homepage should collect a few preliminary details before sending the visitor into full signup. Use a customer-benefit introduction, a short form and one continuation button. The full setup remains one account and one journey. Provider names, carrier filing, internal readiness mechanics and separate service-setup explanations do not belong in the homepage conversion card.

## Email program: prepared direction, not an active campaign

No campaign, scheduled outreach, marketing send or purchased audience is activated by these documents. Billing remains off. Sender delivery and a real customer lifecycle have not been verified by the local onboarding fixtures.

When an email program is explicitly selected for delivery, begin with three useful lifecycle purposes:

1. **Resume setup:** help an eligible person return to their saved progress, with one relevant next step. Never assume an abandoned lead agreed to a subscription, sales call or activated service.
2. **Try the front desk:** send a short example relevant to the owner's chosen goal and invite them to test their own saved rules. State the current service scope accurately.
3. **Learn what changed:** explain a released improvement that solves the person's stated problem. Avoid broad feature announcements without a useful next action.

Marketing emails require the product's explicit audience eligibility rule: verified ownership, recorded affirmative email preference and no current suppression. This is our product policy, not a statement that US federal law universally requires prior opt-in. Marketing preference is optional and must not prevent account creation. Email preference does not authorize texts or calls. Transactional account-access mail must not be silently repurposed as a marketing sequence.

Before sending, OM and Marketing must have a usable verified sender, truthful sender/subject information, the business's valid postal address, a working unsubscribe route and durable suppression honored by every sending path. The FTC explains that commercial-email rules also cover business-to-business messages and that mixing account content with promotion can change a message's primary purpose. Source: [FTC CAN-SPAM business guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), reviewed October 22, 2026.

## Next evidence to collect

- Which stated goal predicts a useful first test, rather than only a form submission?
- Where do visitors stop: first form, email proof, business rules, review or activation?
- Can a visitor explain what they can use now and what is still unavailable before activation?
- Do opted-in owners find a specific example or setup reminder useful? Measure replies and completed next steps, alongside unsubscribe and complaint signals, before expanding volume.

Do not call the funnel effective, the audience subscribed, or the customer live until the corresponding observed result exists.
