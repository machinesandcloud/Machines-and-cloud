These choices follow existing operator evidence rather than claiming twelve lines solve every operating problem. [Live-demo research](../RESEARCH-live-demo.md) O1/O4 supports overflow intake with a human escape; O3 supports truthful arrival windows; O5/O6 supports short answers without repeated intake; O7 supports explicit callback-versus-visit next actions; O9 supports owner-specific quote limits. [Stakeholder research](../RESEARCH-stakeholder-forums.md) F1/F3/F4 reinforces clear next actions, realistic times and correction accuracy. Its F9 supports keeping the result simple enough to use without adopting a dispatch suite. Spanish demand remains a product hypothesis: O10 is adjacent Texas translation evidence, not proof of demand for this exact offering.

Source rules remain [the shared practice personas](../netlify/functions/lib/practice-personas.mjs): Bayou diagnostic $89 applied toward approved repair; Katy emergency fee $150 with no defined additive $79 calculation; Westheimer deep clean from $229 with size/room facts required before quoting. Cleaning’s three-bedroom correction does not establish a final deep-clean price formula. Example-shop fees are distinct from Machines & Cloud subscription prices.
