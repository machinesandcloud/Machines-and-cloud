## First delivery decision: verified owner to a resumable activation workspace

The selected vertical slice is **a stranger proves control of their email, creates or resumes exactly one tenant, saves their setup, and reaches an authenticated workspace that tells the truth about what is ready and guides the next useful action.** It must work without a founder call, a hand-edited record or a privately sent portal link.

This precedes extra settings, a general tutorial carousel, billing and broad provisioning. Architect and QA independently found a critical ownership flaw; QA reproduced it. In the retained previously deployed `bb8d515` baseline, an anonymous repeated email overwrites an existing tenant and returns its owner credential. A prettier wizard over that path is not acceptable.

The first slice is a complete safe entry/recovery journey with honest web-only progress where phone/SMS prerequisites are unavailable. It does **not** declare automated phone/SMS service delivery finished. Subsequent channel activation must reuse the same owned workspace and readiness rules rather than add a manual Steve checkpoint.

## Activation and the in-app guide

Keep **identity**, **setup/build**, **owner review**, **test result**, and **channel operation** separate. Suggested customer states are Verify email → Finish shop details → Building → Review facts/needs attention → Ready to test → Channel-specific activation. An operational channel may later be paused or degraded independently. Never collapse all of these into `testing` or a single “live” badge.

The guide now expands three core outcomes (review, private testing and honest activation) into six contextual steps: **Your desk, in one place**, **Make the answers yours**, **Practice the requests you get**, **Protect time you have already booked**, **Choose when customers can use it**, and **Know what needs you each day**. Each step opens the actual setup, rules, private-test, availability or customer-record control. Explain what the owner should observe, show the actual result and offer the next action. A test needs isolation from live bookings/messages; opening a test window alone does not prove this. If safe isolated testing is not yet available, show that limitation and do not mark the task complete. Saved review is evidence of an owner action, not evidence that a provider works.

Channel cards must state the current reason and actor: owner needs to review rules/connect OAuth/verify forwarding; platform setup is unavailable and retrying; carrier review is pending; or the channel is operational based on specific evidence. The customer performs their own OAuth consent, legal attestation and service choices where required. These actions are not founder intervention. Do not promise instant carrier approval or make a payment decision on behalf of the customer.
