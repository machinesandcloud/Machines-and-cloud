# Product team working files

Three roles work from these files:
- **Product Owner (PO)** owns `BACKLOG.md`: vision, what customers need, prioritized epics and features with evidence and rationale. Researches the market continuously and has freedom to add, cut, and reorder.
- **Product Manager (PM)** owns `SPRINT.md`: the current sprint's tickets with acceptance criteria, in priority order, and the status of each; verifies delivered tickets against the criteria and records the result.
- **Engineer** builds tickets from `SPRINT.md`, marks them `delivered` with a note on how to verify, and writes to `DECISIONS.md` when a trade-off was made.

Shared goal: get the first paying Houston home-service shop safely live with calls, texts, bookings and human handoffs, then use its first seven days of real conversations as the evidence for the next product round. Feature work stays frozen until then; billing stays parked.

Ground truth about the product: `../CLAUDE.md` (code map), `../OPERATIONS.md` (how it runs), `../RESEARCH-customer-workflow.md`, `../RESEARCH-customer-journey.md`, `../RESEARCH-servicing.md` (what the market and customers say).
Steve's standing rules: no fake proof or client names, no personal details about the founder beyond one sentence, no meta commentary a real company would not write, prices appear once on the homepage, SEO pages are never deleted, billing is parked for now.
