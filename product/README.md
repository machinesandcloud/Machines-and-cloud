# Product team working files

Three roles work from these files:
- **Product Owner (PO)** is accountable for the final working product and its customer outcomes. Owns `BACKLOG.md`, continuous stakeholder and competitor research, new feature discovery, priorities, and final product acceptance. Turns recurring operator problems into proposed capabilities, challenges the team to improve on competing workflows, and follows a priority through implementation, verification and release. Keeps an unresolved dependency owned and tracked until it is closed; a document, code change or local test alone is not a completed product outcome.
- **Product Manager (PM)** owns the current numbered sprint: bounded delivery tickets, acceptance criteria, dependencies, sequencing and status. Independently exercises the complete customer and owner journey, returns failures to engineering, and supplies the evidence the PO uses to accept or reject the product. Historical sprint files remain records of their original runs.
- **Engineer** implements and fixes those tickets through the full applicable workflow, supplies reviewable changes and meaningful verification, and records trade-offs in `DECISIONS.md`. Distinguishes local results from deployed behavior and actual provider delivery.

Shared goal: deliver a working front desk that helps Houston home-service shops answer customers, book work they can deliver, follow through and keep the owner in control. Get the first paying shop safely live and continue improving the product from operator evidence, competitor research and real conversations. Billing stays parked.
