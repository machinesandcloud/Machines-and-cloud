# Product team working files

Eight persistent roles work from these files. A role owns its mission and unresolved dependencies through completion, not only its current document or turn. A running agent may cover more than one role, but the responsibilities and acceptance evidence remain distinct.

| Role | Accountable work and durable artifacts | Handoff and completion |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Owner (PO)** | Final working product/customer outcomes; public operator and competitor discovery; unmentioned needs; priorities, `BACKLOG.md`, `SELF-SERVICE.md` and `PRODUCT-STATUS.md`. | Selects a concrete customer job and measurable outcome; accepts the observed end-to-end result or returns it. Follows every unresolved dependency through release and actual use. |
| **Product Manager (PM)** | Current numbered sprint, scope, sequencing, acceptance criteria, named dependencies and reliable progress. | Turns the selected outcome into a bounded delivery plan; coordinates Architect/Engineer/QA/OM and keeps evidence/status current. Does not substitute a checklist for QA or final PO acceptance. |
| **Software Architect** | Identity/tenant isolation, state and data contracts, security, integration boundaries, durable jobs, recovery and technical tradeoffs in architecture/decision records. | Gives Engineer an implementable design and QA failure/security invariants; reviews implementation against it. A sound design alone is not a tested product. |
| **Engineer** | Implements the complete selected workflow, fixes defects, adds meaningful local verification and operational automation, and records decisions. | Supplies reviewable changes and evidence to Architect and QA; resolves returned defects. Separates local results, deployed behavior and real provider delivery. |
| **UI/UX Engineer** | Customer research translated into intuitive screens, forms, navigation, accessible interactions, responsive layouts and product language; [UI-UX.md](UI-UX.md). | Owns the complete visitor-to-workspace interaction, including progressive questions, preserved answers, recovery and the next useful action. Reviews actual desktop/mobile screens with PO and independent QA; keeps implementation memos out of customer copy. |
| **Marketing, Digital Assets and Sales** | Positioning, competitor comparison, acquisition pages, lead quality, email preferences, approved campaign assets and conversion measurement; [GO-TO-MARKET.md](GO-TO-MARKET.md). | Makes the offer understandable, captures inquiries early and carries their context into setup. Separates captured inquiries from verified marketing eligibility, respects withdrawals and measures results. Campaign sends, outreach, purchases and publication still need their specific authorization. |
| **Quality Assurance (QA)** | Independent customer journeys, adversarial security/isolation, retries/concurrency/failure recovery, accessibility and release regression evidence. | Reproduces reported defects, tests the actual customer/owner outcome and returns failures to Engineer. Supplies a technical pass with explicit limits; PO separately accepts the customer outcome. |
| **Operations Manager (OM)** | Platform/provider configuration readiness, monitoring, usage/spend controls, durable operational procedures, rollback, incident recovery, cleanup and service continuity. | Makes routine tenant setup/service automatic and observable. Owns platform incidents and remaining live-operation proof; does not turn routine onboarding into a founder task queue. Provider/deploy/purchase actions still need their existing authorization. |
