# Customer platform quality gate

QA owns independent acceptance of the customer workflow, reports reproducible failures to engineering, verifies repairs against the same failure trigger, and keeps release and provider evidence separate. Passing the homepage demonstration does not establish signup, tenant security, activation, delivery or daily owner use.

Current decision, **October 21, 2026, Houston / October 22 UTC:** independent local functional acceptance passes across the recorded journey, targeted Starter repair and final allowance/browser guard. PO and Architect make their separate final decisions. No customer-platform deployment, delivered email, actual tenant AI service or provider acceptance is recorded here. The original `bb8d515` baseline and every returned run below remain historical evidence.

## Current revocable-session and customer-journey proof

For new `onboarding.version=1` workspaces, a current server-stored 24-hour bearer authorizes owner reads, edits, actions, exports and private tests. The portal URL contains no permanent `k`; a permanent key alone cannot grant new-workspace owner access. Invalid, expired or revoked explicit bearer authority fails closed. Sign out revokes the record and clears private UI/browser authority; already-authorized server work may finish. Legacy unmarked shops retain their earlier key path. Browser storage is sessionStorage, not an HttpOnly-cookie migration, and this does not claim team roles or global legacy logout.

MC_LOCAL_NO_KEYS=1 MC_FAKE_MODEL=1 PORT=8895 LOCAL_ADMIN_KEY=qa-customer-admin node --import ./scratchpad/customer-journey-network-guard.mjs scripts/local-server.mjs

## Full customer lifecycle gate

These are required product outcomes, not a claim that every case is currently built. PO selects bounded slices with PM/architect; QA returns failing work to engineering before acceptance.

| Area | Required acceptance | Evidence needed before claiming complete |
|---|---|---|
| Signup and identity | Proven owner, tenant isolation, repeatable recovery, understandable consent and errors, accessible phone/desktop flow. | Above security/API matrix plus actual rendered browser journey. |
| Shop facts | Optional website; owner answers override imported facts; unknown fees/areas stay unknown; draft persists; hours/service/arrival boundaries reviewable. | Synthetic import fixtures including unreadable/hostile website and conflict cases; saved/reloaded portal checks. |
| Automated service delivery | Durable job with per-channel state; retries do not duplicate purchases/assistants/registrations; explicit waiting/failed/action states; no routine founder task. | Injected provider/storage faults and replay/concurrency acceptance; separately approved real provider receipts and cleanup. |
| Readiness and activation | Test mode cannot contact actual customers accidentally; live channels require actual readiness; failed/partial steps never display successful channel service. | Per-channel state and UI tests, callback/calendar/fallback tests, then real number/email/calendar proof. |
| Tutorial | Useful first-run guide with real setup actions; optional skip/reopen; saved progress; no covering controls or focus traps; no manufactured completion. | New-owner phone/keyboard journey through review, test, readiness, daily inbox and pause/resume, including error retry. |
| Daily owner work | See actionable work, distinguish acknowledgment from completed contact, correct details, manage availability, export, pause/resume/cancel accurately. | Browser/API state transitions tied to actual synthetic leads/visits/tasks; no claimed delivery from logs. |
| Operations | Stuck jobs and failures recover automatically where safe; irreversible/ambiguous operations retain reconciliation state; alerts are owned and actionable. | Fault injection, durable retries and reconciliation evidence; real alert receipt remains a separate controlled gate. |
| First live customer | Actual shop consent/rules, verified channel delivery and fallback, honest software integrations and first-week outcomes. | Named-shop gate in PRODUCT-STATUS.md, controlled provider pass and PO acceptance. Local fake or marketing-demo checks are insufficient. |

## Release ownership

QA reports local/fixture/real-model/provider/hosted results separately. PO accepts the customer outcome; architect reviews authority and failure boundaries; engineer repairs and supplies code; PM records dependencies and release scope. No role can waive a failed P0 because a happy path works. Publication still follows the standing one-build approval; billing remains parked and recharge remains off.
