## Product decision

Use three owner situations: **“I’m on a job,” “It’s after hours,” and “My customer speaks Spanish.”** Keep Watch and actual typed Try paths for all three. The current voice slice is the **English after-hours plumbing example**, accepted only after an actual microphone conversation with audible replies. A silent phone transcript must be called a transcript.

The owner should be able to answer: **Will this desk keep the details straight, respect my rules, and show me exactly what I need to do next?** A pleasant exchange or an internally completed animation is insufficient.

This decision follows freshly checked [operator accounts and official competitor experiences](../RESEARCH-live-demo.md). Operators report intake loops, wrong expectations about visits, unusable self-selected times and a preference for office confirmation. These are public anecdotes, not customer validation or evidence that every owner wants AI. Spanish service intake is a hypothesis to demonstrate and test; this research does not quantify Spanish demand in Houston.

## P0: the result must actually be usable

- **First selection works.** Selecting any situation starts its Watch exchange immediately under normal motion preferences, including when the example is already in view. Pause, Play and Replay work; no hidden requirement to discover Play after changing tabs. Reduced motion exposes a complete, readable example. Offscreen/tab changes stop playback and do not restart speech or animation unexpectedly.
- **Completion is visible on a phone.** At 320 and 390 px, the newest reply is readable without clipping and the summary update has a clear nearby cue/action. The visitor can reach the actual summary in one deliberate action. An internal “4 of 4” while the reply is below the viewport or the summary is another long page away fails this requirement. Do not steal scroll while the visitor reads older messages.
- **Watch and live results stay separate.** Scenario/mode/reset changes cancel prior activity and invalidate late replies/events. A scripted successful summary must never remain attached to a new live conversation.
- **Live input works for every situation.** Typed input and explicit Send/Enter use the selected persona; streaming, busy, failure/retry and reset behavior are inspectable. Test the plumber and Spanish paths themselves, not only HVAC and shared controls.
- **Voice means audible interaction where offered.** For this slice, show the voice entry only on the English after-hours plumbing example and label that coverage clearly. Explicit Talk asks for microphone permission and shows listening, connecting, speaking and ended/error states. The visitor can speak, hear the answer, interrupt/stop, read the transcript and see the corresponding owner result. End after the bounded 90-second practice session. Reset, tab/scenario switch and page departure stop the session and release the microphone. Permission denial, unsupported browser, missing configuration or provider failure offers working typing without a silent “connected” state. No automatic microphone start. An optional link from another situation opens the plumbing example without silently starting a call. Spanish remains live typed chat, not a claim of validated Spanish audio.
- **The owner result is truthful.** Use validated structured practice results, including partial unknown fields and later corrections. Do not infer a booking/send from generated reply text. Show the requested action and next human step. Unknown contact information stays unknown; a summary update is not delivery to the real owner.
- **Bridge the owner's language barrier.** In this English owner preview, write the issue and requested timing in English even when the customer speaks Spanish. Retain the Spanish customer conversation and language tag, and preserve names, phone numbers and street addresses exactly. The practice owner-language requirement was made explicit during raw outcome review; a Spanish-only summary does not demonstrate this English-speaking owner's job.
- **Scope is explicit before interaction.** Suggested note: “Practice conversation only. No appointments, phone calls or messages are sent to a shop. Use made-up details.” Browser audio may connect to the practice AI; it does not dial a business or demonstrate a real transfer. Avoid production-looking “Text sent,” “Calendar booked,” “Owner notified” or “On the way” success badges.
- **No avoidable obstruction.** The sales widget, fixed header, mobile menu and microphone controls must not cover the composer, newest reply, stop action, summary link or footer. Keyboard labels, focus, disabled states and error announcements must match actual state.
