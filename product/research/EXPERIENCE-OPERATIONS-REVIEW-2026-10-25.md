Permission and first-lead waits alone do not require a worker. See `thumbtack-connection.mjs:116`, `service-health.mjs:25` and the author evidence.
2. **Operators saw different setup facts from owners — repaired locally.** Original `operator.mjs:65` called `serviceSetupView(c,services)` without the coordinator, installation or lead-source facts used by owner GET. Its later mapping could overwrite a paused or otherwise derived channel status with a lower-level provider state. Seeded owner/manage GET comparison failed in both memory and installed-SDK fixtures: the owner saw retained native setup needing its existing webhook checked; the operator saw the default connection request. The operator now uses the same projection inputs and returns the existing allowlisted lead rows; credentials and provider setup endpoints remain private. A shared sanitized unconfirmed-reply count also exposes an unknown reply even while the native connection remains in its ordinary receiving state. See `operator.mjs:66-90` and `client.mjs:164-170`.
3. **An invalid main receipt could report healthy — repaired locally.** `health.mjs:9-14` previously accepted a future timestamp or a receipt without explicit success/blocks. Its original age calculation could even report negative minutes alongside HTTP200. It now requires a nonfuture timestamp within25 minutes, explicit success and successful block records. A failed service block has a useful public fallback reason rather than `undefined`.
4. **Early signup effort remains a product sequencing decision.** `onboard.mjs:64` requires an owner cell; V2 submission at `onboard.mjs:73` requires trade, services, area, hours and arrival windows before creating a workspace, including a Calendar-only preference. Those facts serve real safety and scheduling rules. PO/UI should distinguish the minimum needed to save an account from the facts needed before a particular service can execute. This slice does not weaken execution readiness or collect more data.
5. **Financial recovery remains separately observable and unaccepted live.** `workspace-billing.mjs:464` records its own completed recovery; `workspace-billing-scheduler.mjs` distinguishes dispatch from completion. Main service health still does not establish a complete financial lifecycle. Preserve the existing financial identities, terms, funding gates and held unknown outcomes. Do not generalize this native-worker repair into a paid-service readiness claim.

Existing `health/leadConnections:lastRun` plus the main `serviceDeliveryRecovery.leadConnections` result answer these with bounded counters and reason codes.
