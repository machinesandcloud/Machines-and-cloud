## Recommendation and limits

Use **$149 / $299 / $499** as the reviewable ladder. Model **1,500 / 3,000 / 6,000 written AI replies**, **100 / 250 / 500 connected hosted call-leg minutes**, and **250 / 750 / 1,500 outbound SMS parts, including owner SMS alerts** per billing period. All plans can include the core web, phone, SMS and Calendar features once their independent activation conditions are met. The top plan adds approved paid-lead automation and its qualification/follow-through, with a proposed maximum 600 native platform replies **within** its 6,000 written replies. Lower plans may receive supported leads and let the owner reply manually. The middle plan’s follow-through refers to existing owner-prepared, consented quote/request message actions; it does not establish an autonomous CRM drip. No per-connector surcharge is proposed. The proposed 600-native sublimit is an engineering suggestion for Root’s review, not a settled catalog term.

These are proposed entitlements, not present capabilities or activated billing. The competitive volumes require cheaper adequate text-model routing and real monthly capacity admission. The current Opus-only implementation and shared daily gates cannot deliver this offer as written. Model quality on the cheaper routes is unverified. Root chooses final names, prices and copy.

Preserve existing free website access as **best effort** under its existing shared safety allowance. Do not turn a free 50/day safeguard into a new paid100/month restriction, or present a shared global pool as reserved capacity. The proposed paid1,500/3,000/6,000 capacity must be funded and admitted separately; any continuing free fallback must keep its prior eligibility and must not incur automatic charges. The company-wide free pool needs its own physical/token cost budget and funding; it is not1,500 reserved replies for every free shop. The model below costs the new paid capacity and allocates shared infrastructure; free-pool spend must also be included in the actual platform budget.

## Written-reply cost target

Hypothesis: at most90% Haiku and10% Sonnet, with a durable **maximum** Sonnet escalation budget. A10% assumption without an admission fence is not a bound.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Base:1.3 physical requests on average,8k input + 500 output/request | $0.001365 | $0.02730 | **$0.0039585** | $0.05460 |
| Stress:2 requests,12k input + 800 output/request | $0.00320 | $0.06400 | **$0.00928** | $0.12800 |

This gives written-model budgets **$5.94/$11.88/$23.75 base**, **$13.92/$27.84/$55.68 stress** for the proposed tiers. At current Opus the same base volumes cost **$81.90/$163.80/$327.60**, before phone/SMS/support. Neither a character/4 guess nor the existing100k-byte native request ceiling enforces these token envelopes.

Target ordinary blended model cost≤**$0.005/completed reply**, while funding the specified stress envelope. The router must preserve answer/safety/grounding quality; it must not use a cheap model to answer beyond its tested scope. A bounded, acknowledged owner handoff is preferable when a valid response cannot be produced within authority. No model switch is authorized by this report.

## Units, limits and owner control

**Sell replies, not leads, initially.** One completed written AI reply is one confirmed customer delivery, deduped by tenant+source+conversation+trigger/customer-message+outbox identity. Tool calls, model retries, webhook retries, corrections saved without a new outbound answer and manual owner replies do not create extra reply units. A model completion, queue entry or pending provider acceptance is not enough. Use the channel’s authoritative delivery evidence: completed web delivery, confirmed publication to the native conversation, or the SMS delivery receipt. Do not imply a read receipt. A delivery-unknown reservation remains pending until reconciliation; it never authorizes a duplicate send or duplicate charge. Known pre-AI spam, duplicate provider events and confirmed failed-before-delivery results use no completed customer unit or receive that unit back. Failed model/provider work can still consume internal supplier exposure, distinct from customer usage.

Count **outbound SMS parts**, including owner SMS alerts, against the displayed allowance; fund inbound cost without deducting it from that allowance. Safety/STOP/service-message reserve and evidence-based abuse safeguards remain separate and cannot silently become a hidden inbound charge. An AI SMS answer consumes one written-reply unit and its outbound parts; there is no hidden second subscription charge. Voice counts **connected hosted call-leg minutes, each connected leg rounded up to 60 seconds**. A human handoff can use two connected legs; those are not free exposure. Avoid double-counting internal vendor components as extra customer call legs. Known unconnected/failed calls are not completed voice minutes. Unknown legs remain reserved until reconciled. Do not bill worst-case safety reservations as actual duration.

If a later lead-count offer is desired, define an **active platform conversation** by tenant+provider+provider business+canonical lead/thread ID per subscription period. One conversation can include a stated maximum of replies, e.g.6; it is not six new leads. A continuing thread used in a new period counts once in that new period. Webhook duplicates, no-response spam and imported-only records do not count as handled leads. Successful AI handling of an unwanted/unwon job is not a supplier lead refund. Current code has no monthly unique-conversation commercial counter; do not present600 replies as600 leads or as unlimited conversations.

Use the actual subscription period as reset anchor, explicitly no rollover. Keep existing customers’ accepted terms/version; catalog edits do not renew allowances or silently migrate subscriptions. Defaults: **no automatic overage spend, no auto-upgrade, no auto-purchase of marketplace leads**. Warn at75/90/100%, show reset/reserved/confirmed values, then pause only new automatic work at a genuine limit. Keep saved Inbox, manual platform replies where authorized, owner takeover, consent/STOP, cancellation and known-operation cleanup available. Extra packs/overage rates require a separate reviewed owner choice and implemented bounded billing; current new-workspace quote says additionalUsageCharges:false.
