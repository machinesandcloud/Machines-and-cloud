# Automatic customer texting delivery

This implementation is local and provider execution stays off. It contains the production Twilio adapter, durable registration state machine, customer authorization, approval polling and delivery proof. The customer-facing actions, signed inbound handler, consent ledger and delivery outbox are integrated by the platform delivery layer. A passing fixture run is not a carrier approval or a live text-delivery result.

## Customer journey

1. The verified customer reviews and privately tests their current business rules, then reviews the legal business identity, a plain-language service-text program generated from their business information, registration/ongoing costs and a separate one-time setup-text agreement. All choices require explicit acceptance. They do not write API fields, sample-message syntax or campaign descriptions themselves.
2. Once the separately authorized dedicated number exists, the worker claims a dependency on that exact number. This claim shares the phone intent's conditional record with number cancellation, so release cannot race child service creation.
3. The worker creates the customer's Messaging Service, Secondary Customer Profile, business and representative EndUsers, registered address and supporting document, entity assignments, profile evaluation/submission, A2P TrustProduct and associated business EndUser, TrustProduct evaluation/submission and Brand registration. It retains every completed resource SID and request receipt.
4. The Brand must be approved before the worker submits the CUSTOMER_CARE Campaign. This program covers requested service questions and appointment updates, with explicit customer consent. It excludes unsolicited missed-call texts, promotions and review campaigns. Sender attachment and managed inbound routing follow actual campaign approval.
5. Carrier review is polled through authenticated Twilio reads. No callback can mark a registration approved. A rejection appears as a customer action with safe field/error codes. Supported legal corrections update existing resources and resubmit the existing Brand/Campaign IDs, with a renewed accepted correction offer. They do not buy a replacement registration. Unsupported business/program migrations remain visible exceptions.
6. After approval and an actual service/sender/webhook verification, the outbox can send the one requested setup text to the accepted owner mobile. The owner replies with its short code. A Twilio Message GET must establish a delivered outbound message and a matching inbound message, exact account, service, sender, recipient, direction, body and bounded creation time. Merely receiving `delivered` in a webhook is insufficient. This ordinary customer possession check does not require founder intervention.
7. The private effective sender becomes available only with that proof, current customer/number ownership, current reviewed/tested business rules, accepted platform delivery policy and a recent approval/route check. Root delivery still checks recipient consent and outbox authority immediately before sending. An accepted, proven texting connection works for `testing` and `live` workspaces independently of website chat. Paused/cancelled customers remain blocked. Editing hours/services requires a fresh rules review/test; this restores the existing accepted connection without purchasing or registering again.

## Durable execution and cancellation

`messaging_activation` is the authoritative per-customer record. Its ID derives from the immutable customer identity rather than a browser retry key. All legal data and the reviewed program are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, a random nonce and the job ID as authenticated associated data. The 32-byte base64 `PLATFORM_DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` may fall back to `CALENDAR_TOKEN_ENCRYPTION_KEY`. This secret must be backed up and retained; rotating it without a migration makes old legal payloads unreadable. Browser DTOs contain no EIN, representative details, provider SIDs, tokens or raw provider errors.

Each provider write has a saved dispatch checkpoint before HTTP starts. Claims use conditional storage. An expired worker lease leads to reconciliation of an unfinished write, not another create. FriendlyName and exact profile relationships are correlation aids; they are not Twilio idempotency guarantees. A missing match, multiple matches or a partial list page holds the operation as uncertain. A known provider effect whose local result write failed is recovered through the original checkpoint. This includes fee-bearing Brand/Campaign calls. An uncertain result can need an operator exception; automatically filing again would risk duplicate fees.

Legal corrections and paid resubmissions are versioned, bounded by the approved offer and accepted again by the customer. Old non-paid evaluation receipts remain in resource history. The supported private-US-EIN path accepts LLCs, corporations and partnerships. Sole proprietors, public companies and special registrations need their own supported path; they are not silently mapped to this one.

Cancellation immediately removes outbound readiness. The worker confirms Campaign deletion, sender detachment and Messaging Service deletion by reading the same resource IDs afterward. Lost delete responses retain receipts and are reconciled. TrustHub/Brand and consent records remain for the retention policy; cancellation does not claim a refund or erase provider logs. Only confirmed child cleanup clears its number dependency. Even a lost parent-dependency claim response has a saved claim-start checkpoint, so cancellation can clear a possibly committed claim. The number itself is released only by the parent phone workflow.

The scheduled worker runs every ten minutes, advances at most three checkpoints for one customer within a 15-second deadline and stores counts/codes in health. The platform's authenticated background continuation can advance the same saved operation sooner. Approval/route checks are cached for up to 24 hours; local pause, cancellation, tenant changes, number cancellation/release and policy shutdown still block delivery immediately. Twilio also enforces current carrier restrictions when a message is submitted. A stale cache fails closed; health reports overdue checks and refuses to call a truncated inspection healthy. The current health inspection is bounded to 50 records and must be expanded with measured load before growing beyond that scope.

An expired signup quote blocks new provider work and hides new setup offers. It does not disable already registered, proven service under its existing accepted terms or prevent safe cancellation cleanup. Changing active usage terms needs the appropriate customer communication and acceptance; this code does not invent allowance or overage policy.

## Platform gates and integration contract

Every production request requires `MESSAGING_ACTIVATION_ENABLED=true`, the exact `MESSAGING_ACTIVATION_APPROVED_VERSION`, a structurally valid `MESSAGING_ACTIVATION_OFFER_JSON`, `SERVICE_DELIVERY_ENABLED=true`, a `SERVICE_WORKER_KEY` of at least 32 characters, a private-data encryption key, Twilio credentials and the approved Primary Customer Profile SID. `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN` is required for signed webhooks even when API keys authenticate REST requests. The provider checks the Primary Profile is actually `twilio-approved` before continuing. No gate was enabled by this work.

The offer specifies version, USD registration and recurring Campaign charges, correction charges/budget, usage disclosure, authority wording, fee-schedule link and expiry. Amounts must be confirmed before enabling it. The adapter does not claim a provider-enforced charge cap or fabricate a charged-amount receipt.

- Customer actions: `requestMessagingActivation`, `correctMessagingActivation`, `cancelMessagingActivation`. The verified actor comes from the owner session. Request fields include current configuration revision, submission ID, offer version/digest, generated program digest, legal fields and five separate true acceptances (`authority`, `acceptedCosts`, `attestLegalAccuracy`, `attestConsent`, `testPhoneConsent`). Corrections also bind the operation and submission revision.
- Owner/admin DTO: `messagingActivationStatus`. `buildMessagingProgram` supplies the text to review; customers do not author technical carrier filing fields.
- Owner conversation history: `managedSMSConversations` in `managed-history.mjs` projects current-tenant durable inbound/reply pairs and retained history into ordinary SMS conversations, including queued and failed customer requests. It deduplicates message SIDs privately, exposes up to 40 messages for each of the latest 60 contacts by default, normalizes Twilio RFC-2822 dates, labels replies awaiting delivery, and omits provider/session/private registration metadata. Provider references, authorization values and identifiable EIN patterns are redacted from message text. Reused workspace slugs cannot expose a prior owner's tenant incarnation.
- Private delivery: `messagingSender` returns exact service/number IDs only after readiness. `pendingMessagingProbe` exposes the separately accepted setup-text instruction to the internal outbox. `recordMessagingProbe` verifies both actual Message resources. Neither belongs in a public DTO.
- Signed inbound: `findMessagingClientByNumber` identifies the unique current tenant owning that exact retained number, including paused/cancelling customers so STOP still persists. The private `messagingInboundBinding` supplies the service/number IDs and registration creation time. Verified Message reads must match that service and occur after that registration began, preventing old messages for a reused number entering a new tenant. The handler must record STOP/START/HELP before any AI or delivery readiness decision. The sender remains unavailable while paused or cancelled.
- Recovery and parent release: `continueMessagingActivation`, `recoverMessagingActivations`, `messagingRecoveryHealth`, `requestMessagingCancellationForPhone`, `messagingCleanupStatus`. The latter never authorizes releasing a number while child cleanup is uncertain.

Keyword policy: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, ALTO and PARAR suppress service texts; START, UNSTOP and EMPEZAR only remove a previous suppression; HELP, INFO and AYUDA provide help. A keyword restart does not create new recurring appointment consent. All shared senders must check the separate consent ledger. Twilio's default English opt-out protection remains in addition to application-side Spanish handling. Advanced Opt-Out custom keywords are configured in the Console; the public Services API does not offer a documented field for enabling those custom lists. Campaign keyword declarations are not presented as changing provider Advanced Opt-Out settings.
