## What happens after Create in the published product

1. [`onboard.js`](../onboard.js) saves the verified owner's draft, validates the current answers and POSTs them to [`/api/onboard`](../netlify/functions/onboard.mjs). The email comes from the authenticated session, not an editable ownership field.
2. The handler checks name/business/contact cell, normalizes business answers and reserves one tenant through [`ensureVerifiedAccount`](../netlify/functions/lib/accounts.mjs). An existing account is returned without replacing its settings. Interrupted projections resume the same reservation.
3. The created workspace has `onboarding.version=1`, status `testing`, a deterministic `owner_answers` build and **inactive web/voice/SMS channels**. No model crawl, number purchase, subscription, carrier submission or customer notification happens during creation. The legacy provisioning/build workers explicitly reject this workspace version.
4. The browser displays the returned private portal link. [`desk.js`](../desk.js) loads the authenticated owner view, setup/connection facts, records and a contextual walkthrough. Walkthrough completion is a local preference, not activation evidence.
5. The owner must currently review rules, complete a private live-AI rehearsal and explicitly activate web chat. Installation on the owner's website is still a copy/paste task. Saved phone/text/Calendar preferences do not start service jobs.
6. The published Connections area separately requests a number, phone authority, texting registration and Google authorization. Each existing engine continues its **own saved intent** automatically; none coordinates the complete selected journey from the saved preferences.

## Information and actions the journey must cover

Collect shared answers once and reuse them. Ask conditional questions only for the selected service. A preference is not a purchase or legal attestation.

| Information or customer action | Published collection / actual requirement | Unified treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Name, work email, business name, main objective, marketing preference | Homepage inquiry carries context into verified setup. Email verification establishes account ownership. Marketing remains a separate optional choice. | Preserve this early capture; do not infer marketing permission from verification or service signup. |
| Contact cell | Required during signup; server normalizes a US `+1` number. It is not proof of receipt, SMS consent or permission to forward this line. | Reuse for callbacks and explicitly agreed setup checks. Explain when a separate business line is needed. |
| Trade, services, service area, per-day hours, arrival windows | Signup presents these, but most are optional there; [`setupReadiness`](../netlify/functions/lib/setup.mjs) needs them before activation. Blank answers can therefore create a blocked workspace. | Validate the selected service's operational minimum before presenting the final connection review. Permit saving an incomplete draft; explain the exact missing answer. Never invent hours, prices, availability or services. |
| Prices, FAQ, Spanish, after-hours/safety/handoff instructions | Collected in the questionnaire; defaults exist for safety rules and arrival windows. Review/test proof is tied to a configuration digest. | One readable business summary and relevant rehearsal. Changed answers invalidate stale proof, not the tenant identity or its existing resources. |

Voice defaults to forwarding an existing line and rejects a missing line or one equal to the owner cell. Save each underlying engine's authority separately. Unavailable/expired offers must not collect financial consent. | SMS legal identity | Only the later texting dialog collects legal name, EIN, supported type, registered address, public HTTPS website and representative. Supported path is US LLC/corporation/partnership with EIN, 50 states/DC. Website is optional for free signup but required for this texting path. | Conditional secure form in the same journey. | SMS costs, legal accuracy, authority, customer-consent process and setup-text agreement | Existing texting engine requires these distinct affirmative facts and exact program/offer digests. | Google account and consent | Owner-session-bound OAuth/PKCE with `calendar.events.owned` and `calendar.calendars.readonly`; encrypted refresh token; primary calendar only. Callback is `/api/google/callback`. Published UI also asks “Finish connecting.” | Customer chooses Google account and grants access. Return to the same journey; a validated same-session/state return can complete automatically rather than add an unnecessary finish task. SMS needs carrier approval and a two-way setup-text reply. | Present short customer checks only when the background stage is ready. A number, queued job or accepted provider request never means service is live. |
| Website installation | Current product generates an embed snippet and permits the saved website/additional origins. It has no website-host installation integration. | Keep a clear install step or later build a customer-authorized host/plugin connection. Additional optional inputs such as backup contact, review/payment links, lead-source integrations and scheduling software should appear when their supported functionality is enabled. No current payment-card collection/subscription is required; billing remains parked. The supported Google implementation writes saved visits and their changes/cancellations; **it does not import external busy time or provide two-way scheduling**. Blocking already-booked time is still a customer task until that separate capability is built. Calendar event time zone is currently `America/Chicago`.

## Actual automation added locally

[`service-onboarding.mjs`](../netlify/functions/lib/service-onboarding.mjs) now coordinates the existing engines; it does not duplicate their provider logic.

```text
verified workspace + reviewed/tested current business answers
  → one current combined service review and encrypted legal input if SMS selected
  → durable accepted plan / recoverable encrypted admission
  → existing number request → existing number worker → confirmed reserved number
  → automatically create selected existing voice and SMS intents
  → their workers configure routing / file and poll carrier registration
  → customer-specific call/text checks → current child delivery readiness
```

Phone-only setup does not require the SMS/delivery gate; texting-only setup does not require voice. Google consent remains a separate customer-controlled account connection within the same journey. All requested service offers must be available at acceptance; when today's gates are off the coordinator rejects paid setup before saving legal/financial acceptance. Account creation and preferences still work.

The plan binds owner/tenant, business revision, lifecycle, selected channels, exact current number/voice/text/program digests and provider credential binding. The worker rechecks these before each handoff. It persists preparation/acknowledgment and the exact child identities; an invoked request with an unknown outcome is reconciled against the existing engine's durable intent rather than creating a replacement purchase. A missing primary before dispatch can be projected from the encrypted admission; a missing primary after dispatch is retained as an exception, never reconstructed into fresh authority.

Safe **pre-dispatch** changes support renewed owner review without buying anything. Once provider work exists, changed terms/rules expose the existing connection actions rather than silently replace resources or file another registration. Cancellation is durable, reaches retained children before number release, and retains uncertain outcomes. Already-admitted concurrent work can finish; this coordinator does not claim a cross-engine transaction that prevents every in-flight effect. A known denial before invoking a child clears only that unused preparation marker.

The queue admits at most 25 ordinary pending setups and inspects at most 50 entries including reserved cancellation work. Verified active/cancelled plans leave the queue while their primary receipts remain. Later cancellation re-admits that receipt. The coordinator scheduler runs every two minutes and visits at most five **distinct** candidates under one shared 12-second admission deadline. A failed candidate consumes its turn without starving the rest. Fifty retained entries therefore need ten nominal waves/20 minutes when those waves fit their budgets. The scheduler stops admitting another candidate after observing deadline expiry; it does not forcibly interrupt in-flight storage work. Health allows the normal first 25-minute scheduler grace, then reports stale automatic work, missing post-dispatch receipts, unresolved requests or worker failures. A known customer review retains its reason while the worker reports a successful waiting outcome; merely waiting for the customer's correction/test/pause is not a platform outage by age alone. Selection IDs and dispatch/tenant details stay out of public and health receipts. These are implementation bounds and nominal timing, not proven live capacity, per-account spending caps or customer plan allowances.

Required integration: owner-only combined request/cancel API, owner journey DTO, dedicated authenticated background dispatch and scheduled recovery, one-review Connections UI and shared contact/routing fields. Root owns those hooks; independent QA must exercise the actual combined tree and e997-based candidate before this is accepted. [Focused coordinator/recovery proof](../scratchpad/service-onboarding-scheduler-author-final.json) passes 33/33 locally, including actual four-workspace and 50-retained scheduler waves, shared-deadline/failed-candidate handling and scheduled customer-review health. It uses memory storage/synthetic dependencies with physical networking denied. Independent actual-handler/provider-interception proof is separate. Neither establishes a real number purchase, live carrier registration or delivered customer call/text.

## Remaining decisions and acceptance

- Keep the seven platform execution gates off. Before paid enablement, implement/accept the exact-tenant cohort boundary or isolated-account alternative, pending-work census, account-level exposure controls, approved current offers/allowances, secret custody, and real provider cleanup/delivery proof described in the readiness packet. The new coordinator does not supply those missing commercial/platform controls.
- Existing business-line forwarding requires an action in a carrier/phone system the customer controls; the current implementation has instructions, not a universal carrier-management integration. Direct use of the new number avoids that forwarding task. It does not port the existing number, and forwarding calls does not move existing-number SMS to the new sender.
- Carrier decisions, legal corrections, mailbox/MFA and Google consent cannot be truthfully replaced with automatic success. Automate preparation, submission, polling, recoverable work and clear next steps around those decisions; never send the customer into provider dashboards for routine platform setup.
- Run local interruption/concurrency/privacy/cancellation and responsive journey checks on the complete candidate. Then separately accept one real verified signup/return, one controlled shop's phone/texting delivery, Calendar create/change/cancel, cleanup and first-week operations. The earlier hosted signup attempt stopped at authentication and did not prove these outcomes.
- A useful completion screen must distinguish **your workspace is ready**, **connections are being prepared**, **waiting for carrier review**, **one action needed**, and **verified live**. The owner should always have one next useful action and can safely leave while background work continues. No founder-created tenant, manually edited resource ID or promised callback is a normal step.
