# Demo expansion and public pricing QA

October 22, 2026. This review is local. It does not authorize publication, AI requests, microphone access, provider calls, messages, billing changes or customer outreach.

## Acceptance contract

The Watch experience must remove the message-count label and deliver twelve ordered messages in each of the three examples. At 1440, 390 and 320 pixels, sequentially selecting plumbing, Spanish and HVAC must start playback immediately, reveal every message with the newest bubble readable, update the owner view from facts already supplied and finish without a fake booking or notification claim. The owner preview must remain one action away on mobile. Reduced motion must immediately expose the complete exchange for every example. Pause, explicit resumption, Replay, offscreen and hidden-document behavior must continue to work.

The added exchanges should demonstrate concrete owner questions rather than claim to cover every possible customer problem. QA will independently review area, known fees versus unknown total prices, urgency, request-only timing, corrected details, callback versus visit intent, Spanish customer language and the English owner handoff. Scripted examples establish illustration and rendering, not new live-model quality or actual service delivery.

Typing and voice remain separate from Watch playback. No microphone or typed request may start on page load or scenario selection. HVAC and Spanish retain typed practice; only English plumbing exposes the explicit voice control. Injected transport fixtures must continue to prove selected-persona requests, retry and draft recovery, reset/scenario invalidation, late-event rejection and a usable End control. They do not prove an audible provider call.

The public offer is Coverage at $99 and Follow-through at $249. Existing plan IDs and legacy records stay compatible; this decision does not change included units, overage rates or billing activation. QA will compare homepage cards, structured data, onboarding selections, public backend projection and sales planning output. Legacy Pro query links and saved drafts must fall back to a valid public selection without a null error or silent subscription; existing Pro workspaces must remain reachable. Free setup, no card, no automatic trial and separately gated channels must remain clear. Older source reports and raw test artifacts remain historical evidence.

## Content and visual assessment

QA reviewed the actual twelve-message scripts and the per-message owner states. HVAC distinguishes diagnostic fees from unquoted repairs, refuses an exact arrival promise, collects a preferred window, replaces the old phone number and preserves the customer’s control over alternate windows. Plumbing distinguishes an urgent callback from a booked visit, declines to invent additive charges or a ten-minute guarantee, replaces the old number, respects call-only/no-text preferences and requires conversation before dispatch. Spanish collects sizing before its starting-price answer, refuses an exact-time guarantee, preserves Spanish customer dialogue, updates two bedrooms to three while retaining two bathrooms and 1,000 square feet, and presents the English owner handoff with an unconfirmed Saturday window.

QA visually inspected the complete 1440 px and 320 px plumbing views, the 390 px Spanish conversation and the clean 390 px pricing viewport. The corrected summary and newest replies are readable; the mobile owner-preview action remains accessible, and the sales launcher does not cover the conversation. The price heading and first card remain legible without an overlapping focused skip link. PO independently owns the broader visual/content acceptance. ## Limits and handoff

This acceptance covers the expanded scripted demonstration, its existing fixture-based practice controls, and the selected public pricing presentation. It does not establish new live-model quality, an audible voice call, provider delivery, an actual mailbox/signup, billing economics, invoice correctness or production behavior. The examples illustrate specific service-shop pain points; they do not prove coverage of every industry or dealer workflow. Phone, SMS, integrations and paid activation remain separately gated.

Coverage and Follow-through keep the prior $99/$249 amounts and existing planning units/rates. Pro is no longer a new public preference; legacy records remain compatible. The retained internal research tables are historical comparisons, not current public offers. Billing remains off. The separately returned sales-model issues still require the separately proposed targeted real-AI review; none was executed here.

Root owns the required package/build checks and release decision. No deployment is included in this QA acceptance.
