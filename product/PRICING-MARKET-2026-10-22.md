# Pricing market review — October 22, 2026

Owner: Product Owner. Research accessed October 22, 2026 UTC / October 21 Houston time. Scope: public official prices and packaging for a Houston service company with one to five crews. No accounts, sales enquiries, calls, purchases, paid inference or deployment were performed. This review changes no runtime prices or billing settings.

## Decision

**Keep the current website workspace free and billing off. Do not launch the current phone/SMS bundles as priced.** The three monthly amounts are plausible market price points, but the included usage is financially risky and the premium service outcomes are unproven. A lower allowance at the same price improves cost exposure; it does not make the offer more competitive.

For a future paid pilot, test **two offers at $99 and $249 per month**, with genuinely different outcomes, rather than three tiers differentiated mainly by minutes. Withhold the $449 Pro sales offer until both an incremental customer outcome and high-volume costs are demonstrated. These are hypotheses for a later package decision, not approved new customer terms. Leave the current three amounts as uncharged plan preferences until that decision is made.

**There is no justified phone-service premium today.** We have not accepted operational tenant phone/SMS delivery or observed willingness to pay. Bilingual answering and a website widget alone cannot justify a premium: lower-priced competitors already advertise them. The current published `b65c0f4` workspace is web-first; its bounded hosted check did not establish delivered verification mail, a completed live signup, tenant AI quality or phone/SMS activation. The newer sales adviser is local and unpublished. These stages remain separate from market research.

## Customer value and package hypothesis

The initial buyer is a one-to-five-crew Houston shop that misses some calls while working or after hours, not a company replacing a staffed dispatch center. Measure **qualified requests the owner can actually act on, correct next actions and customer-confirmed outcomes**, not gross call counts or a hypothetical technician salary avoided.

Existing [public operator research](../RESEARCH-stakeholder-forums.md) supports clearer call-versus-visit commitments, owner-controlled capacity and correct customer/property context. Those self-reported accounts do not validate a dollar premium or Houston willingness to pay. A solo electrician's objection to paying over $200 for a side business is an individual constraint, not a market-wide ceiling.

|---|---|---|

| **Pro — do not offer yet** | A future multi-crew/integration/high-volume service needs its own proven job and support boundary. | The defensive **800 min + 1,500 segments at $449** scenario remains internal analysis, not an approved package or a reason to sell Pro today. |

Potential overages **$0.49/min and $0.05/outbound SMS segment** are cost-control hypotheses only. A segment is not a message: Unicode and length can multiply carrier units. Customer-facing counters must explain this, while the cost ledger reconciles inbound messages, owner notifications, retries and failed sends. Do not silently turn the current logical-message promises into segment billing. No unlimited-AI/web claim; retain an understandable reply allowance and exhaustion behavior.

The [Architect's cost model](PRICING-COSTS-2026-10-22.md) assumes $0.10/$0.18/$0.30 voice cost bands, not observed invoices. At the $0.18 planning band, full current Pro usage models **$506.77 cost on $449 revenue**, before optional human-support reserve. Even reduced candidate allowances model only **44.18% / 53.90% / 53.71% service contribution** for $99/$249/$449 before that extra support reserve. Thus the candidates improve exposure but do **not** meet a proposed 60% contribution gate. Do not make them a paid commitment before measuring the deployed stack and changing cost/scope as necessary.

An alternative is **$49 platform + metered voice/SMS**, initially modeled at $0.49/min/$0.05/segment: voice-only bills become $122.50 at 150 min and $245 at 400. It reduces bundled-usage exposure, but makes bills less predictable and compares poorly with volume-rich rivals. Prefer two understandable bundles if measured economics support them; otherwise optimize the stack or narrow the service before launch. A required paid setup service contradicts the self-service goal. No mandatory setup fee is recommended; disclose any unavoidable actual carrier/pass-through fee before authorization. Do not offer annual lock-in until delivery, retention and refund/cancellation handling are proven.

## Decision gates and accountable next actions

1. **Architect + Engineer: measured service economics.** Pin voice/STT/TTS/model configuration; reconcile actual provider receipts and segment counts, including Spanish/Unicode, inbound, owner alerts, retries, transfers and reply AI. Model 25/50/100% utilization and 95th-percentile observed cost. A proposed launch gate is at least **60% service contribution after recurring infrastructure/payment/support reserves**, with positive marginal contribution in the stress case. This is our operating target, not an industry fact. Fix metering before selecting quotas.
2. **OM + QA: deliver the promised service.** Separately authorize and prove tenant activation, real number/call handling, delivered notifications, callback/visit distinction, booking constraints, emergency recovery, pause/cancellation and budget exhaustion. A provider key, demo call or successful webhook is insufficient. Current inactive channels remain free of subscription charges.
3. **PO + PM: test willingness to pay with the target buyer.** Propose a separately authorized, consented pilot with **five one-to-five-crew shops**, including at least two Houston/Texas operators if recruited. Show the real product and competing public offers; test $99/$249 as explicit alternatives. Record selection/refusal and reasons, not a leading “would this help?” answer. A useful initial decision threshold is three accepted paid renewals after a full month at the disclosed offer, with both tiers represented before claiming either validated. This is a small falsification test, not statistical market proof; no recruitment or billing is authorized here.
4. **PO: prove the premium outcome.** For each pilot, retain the starting call mix, agreed action, owner contact/visit completion and corrections. Separate “request captured,” “owner acknowledged,” “customer reached” and “job completed.” Count attributable value only from that shop's evidence; do not borrow job values, competitor conversion statistics or invented revenue recovery. Return to the cheaper Coverage proposition if Follow-through does not improve an outcome the owner values.
5. **Root + PO: approve a concrete pricing change later.** Only after these gates choose final units, allowances, fees and cancellation terms; synchronize homepage, signup, sales facts, billing and receipts. This review authorizes none of those mutations. Prices/billing remain unchanged, recharge stays off, and deployment needs its separate approval.

## Review boundary

This is fresh official market research plus a product recommendation and clearly labeled arithmetic/cost scenarios. It is not competitive functional testing, customer validation, measured production economics or acceptance of a paid service. Primary URLs above should be rechecked before a pricing release because vendor offers and units change.
