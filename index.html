Where the jobs go

The 10 pm call

The AC quits on a Friday in July. The homeowner calls three companies. The one that answers gets the job. Voicemail does not get a callback.

The quote request that waits

A web form comes in at 6 pm. Someone reads it at 9 the next morning. By then the customer has booked with whoever replied in five minutes.

The same six questions

Do you cover Pearland. How much is a diagnostic. Are you open Saturday. Your dispatcher answers them forty times a day instead of dispatching.
