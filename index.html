The rules

What it never does, and what it always does

It never

  • Quotes a price you did not write down
  • Promises a time you did not set
  • Pretends to be a person
  • Gives advice on gas, electrical, or safety beyond "call us or 911"
  • Asks only happy customers for reviews, or offers anything for one

It always

  • Says it is the front desk and that a person will confirm
  • Takes a name and number before anything else
  • Sends emergencies to you first
  • Logs every conversation you can read
  • Stops texting the moment someone says stop
