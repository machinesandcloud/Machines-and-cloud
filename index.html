Ask us directly ↗

Do I have to change my number?+

No. Keep your number and carrier. Forward missed calls to the front desk, or have it answer first and send the calls that need you. We set that up with you.

What can it say about prices and arrival times?+

Only the fees and arrival windows you set. It checks recorded availability before booking and never promises an exact arrival time. For work booked elsewhere, add unavailable time in your portal; it does not read your other calendar’s busy times.

What happens with emergencies or unusual questions?+

You set the emergency rules and the questions that always go to a person. The front desk follows those instructions, collects the contact details, and alerts you instead of making up an answer.

Does it work with my scheduling software?+

It can add bookings to Google Calendar when connected, or send the booking to you to enter yourself. Jobber and Housecall Pro use customer CSV exports; they are not connected live.

What about Angi, Thumbtack and Google leads?+

Calls from Google ads forward like other calls. We can set up Angi and Thumbtack lead intake with you. When a lead includes a phone number, the front desk can text the customer. Replies inside the Thumbtack app still come from you.

How soon can I start, and whose number sends texts?+

We set up the front desk and test it with you before switching it on. Texting from your own number needs carrier registration, which can take one to six weeks. An approved shared sender can carry your shop’s name while your registration is pending.

Is there a contract, and who owns my data?+

It is month to month. Cancel from your portal. Your customer details, leads and conversations belong to your shop, and you can export them. We never use one shop’s conversations to run another shop’s desk.
