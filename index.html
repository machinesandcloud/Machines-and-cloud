After the job

The texts that get you the review and the next job

At dispatch
Hi Maria, Ray is on the way. Window is 8 to 12. Please move the car out of the driveway so he can reach the unit.

No-shows drop when the customer knows who is coming and what to prepare.

Same day, after the job
Thanks for choosing Bayou Air & Heat. If today went well, a quick review helps a lot: g.page/bayou-air/review

Sent to every customer, no gating, no rewards. That is the Google rule and the FTC rule.

48 hours later
How is the AC holding up? Reply here if anything is off and Ray will call you.

One nudge, then it stops. Unhappy answers go to you, not to Google.

Day 14, unsold estimate
Hi Jen, the EV charger estimate is still good through Friday. Want to book it, or should I close it out?

One follow-up on an open quote, with an expiry, then it stops chasing.
