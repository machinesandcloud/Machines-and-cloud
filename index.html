Example
Customer

My AC runs but won’t cool. Do you come to Katy, and what’s the call-out fee?

Front desk

Yes, we serve Katy. The diagnostic visit is $89, applied to the repair if you go ahead.

Customer

Does that cover the whole repair? I don’t want a surprise price.

Front desk

The $89 covers the diagnostic, not the whole repair. The technician quotes the repair on site; I can’t give you a repair total before that.

Customer

Could I request tomorrow at exactly 2 pm?

Front desk

We don’t promise exact arrival times. Our usual windows are 8 to 12 or 12 to 5; which would you like to request?

Customer

Tomorrow, 12 to 5 works. I’m Alex, (713) 555-0142, at 12 Oak St, Katy.

Front desk

Thanks, Alex. The preview has your AC problem, contact details and tomorrow’s 12-to-5 request. That window still needs Ray’s confirmation.

Customer

Actually, use (713) 555-0143 instead. The address is still 12 Oak St.

Front desk

Updated your number to (713) 555-0143. The Katy address and tomorrow’s 12-to-5 request stay the same.

Customer

If that window doesn’t work, ask me before choosing a different one.

Front desk

Added: ask you before changing the window. Ray’s preview now has the corrected number and your preferred time; no visit is confirmed.

Let’s talk ↗

Do I have to change my number?+

Website chat leaves your existing number and carrier as they are. Choose the connections you want in the same setup. Phone answering isn’t available yet, and we won’t change your number during signup.

What can it say about prices and arrival times?+

Only the fees and arrival windows you set. New requests use recorded availability, and the front desk never promises an exact arrival time. Add work booked elsewhere as unavailable time; it does not read your other calendar’s busy times.

What happens with emergencies or unusual questions?+

You set the safety instructions and the questions that need your judgment. The front desk follows those instructions and records urgent requests in your workspace. Phone and text alerts are not active, so check your requests regularly.

Does it work with my scheduling software?+

Manage availability and customer requests in your workspace, and download customer details as a CSV. Keep work booked elsewhere up to date here. Google Calendar, Jobber and Housecall Pro aren’t connected.

What about Angi, Thumbtack and Google leads?+

Your website front desk can answer visitors and collect job details. Connections to Angi, Thumbtack and Google leads aren’t available yet; keep replying through those platforms as usual.

How do I get started?+

Tell us about your business, verify your email and add the answers your customers need. Then review your front desk, try a private conversation and activate website chat when it’s ready. Phone, text and calendar preferences stay together in your workspace; those connections aren’t available yet.

Is there a contract, and who owns my data?+

Creating a workspace starts no paid subscription. Your customer details, requests and conversations belong to your shop, and you can export them. We never use one shop’s conversations to run another shop’s desk.
