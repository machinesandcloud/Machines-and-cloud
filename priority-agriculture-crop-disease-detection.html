The task

What crop disease detection could help with

A useful flag gives the crop team a location, visible evidence and enough context to choose an inspection or treatment review. Compare healthy plants, similar symptoms and uncertain images as well as confirmed disease.

  • Who reviews it: Crop management teams
  • What to measure: Yield protection, treatment timing, loss avoidance
  • Example test: One-crop disease detector with review queue and treatment recommendation workflow.

Example steps

How to evaluate crop disease detection

Follow the information through each step. For crop disease detection, the reviewer needs to see the source, understand the suggestion and know when to keep using the current process.

1

Capture

Start with sample imagery or events that a knowledgeable reviewer can check.

2

Infer

Compare model suggestions with examples your team has already reviewed.

3

Flag

Keep the source information beside each suggestion so a reviewer can check it.

4

Review

Agree which cases need a person to review and what that person should see.

5

Learn

Record corrections and test again before changing how the work is handled.

Controls

Before anyone relies on it

Decide who can view the source information and who reviews crop disease detection results. Test how the team corrects a wrong result and returns to its current process when information is missing.

Measurement

What to compare

Compare yield protection, treatment timing, loss avoidance with the current process. Review missed cases, incorrect suggestions and the time people spend checking the output before deciding whether the approach helps.
