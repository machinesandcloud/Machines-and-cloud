# Machines & Cloud

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

US home-service business owners and small teams: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, cleaning, landscaping and related trades. They are often on a job or using a phone and need to understand a request and act without re-entering the same information. The operator manages service exceptions and customer support.

## Product Purpose

Keep customer inquiries moving from the first answer to a useful, retained next step. One business profile supplies services, service area, hours, prices and handoff rules to the supported front-desk channels. The customer workspace joins setup, conversations, requests and owner follow-through; the admin portal exposes actionable exceptions.

## Operating Context

Public website → short inquiry form → verified email access → saved business details → private practice → the selected channel's connection and delivery check → customer request → owner action → follow-up, account management and support. Customers choose channels and grant their own provider permissions. Configuration and routine retries belong to the existing backend, not a founder's manual task list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current implementation, published behavior and actual provider acceptance are different evidence states. `product/LAUNCH-CONTROL.json` is authoritative for current production, launch decisions, commercial holds and the next unresolved provider outcome. This file does not grant activation or release authority. The published guide covers website chat, phone answering, texting, Google Calendar, Thumbtack and Angi. Additional researched platforms are discovery options until their actual connector is implemented, authorized and verified.

The existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend and Netlify Functions, retained operation journals and background workers remain the delivery foundation. A setup choice, tutorial completion, saved configuration or green health response is not proof that a customer's request or reply was delivered. Retain customer work through slow, failed and uncertain responses. No duplicated queues, silently changed prices/rights or automatic new purchases.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Machines & Cloud name and recognizable blue/navy logo identity. The user specifically likes the original high-quality trade photography, clean appearance and simple interactions. Public copy should be concrete, confident and useful to a working shop owner. Explain relevant requirements at the decision where they matter. Do not turn marketing pages into implementation notes or invent testimonials, customer counts, performance promises or provider availability.

## Evidence on Hand

`product/GUIDED-SERVICE-SETUP-2026-10-25.json`, its source-bound QA and actual publication receipts; previous controlled website proof and retained customer/provider test results; existing competitor/forum research. These have explicit source and scope limits. Installed skills are tools for the work, not evidence that a delivered service is ready.

## Product Principles

- Ask once, retain the answer and reuse it across channels.
- Put the customer's next useful action before optional settings.
- Show real progress and recovery rather than optimistic success.
- Make routine setup and service automatic; reserve permissions and business choices for the owner.
- Accept the complete customer outcome with independent QA, then distinguish publication from actual provider delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard use, meaningful landmarks, visible focus and field errors, clear status announcements, responsive layouts from 320px, and reduced motion. English and Spanish customer conversation quality requires its own evidence; translated interface copy does not establish it.
