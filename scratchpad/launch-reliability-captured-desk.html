Commands you can text back
Every alert names the one reply it needs: ok REF to acknowledge, called REF when you have reached someone who asked for a call, otw REF on the way, done REF when the job is finished, cancel REF. A bare ok, called, otw, or done works when only one job fits. Also invoice REF 450 if they still owe you (payment reminders on day 3 and 7), paid REF to stop them, quote REF 1450 when you sent a quote. Follow-up texts only go out between 9 am and 9 pm (noon to 9 pm on Sunday).
