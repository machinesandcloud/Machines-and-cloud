Formula, assumptions and limits

Service contribution = monthly price − voice − outbound/inbound/owner SMS − reply AI − fixed service allocation − website AI reserve − payment fees − selected support reserve.

    Each voice band includes Vapi, transcription, voice generation, language model and telephony. No confirmed paid tenant voice provider/call invoice is available. The SMS mix and token usage are unmeasured. No cache saving is guaranteed.

    Excludes taxes, acquisition/marketing, one-time registration/setup, refunds/disputes, any extra Stripe Billing fee and general company overhead. This is service contribution, not company profit. Prices and taxes need separate approval before paid launch. Shared AI caps must be disclosed separately.
